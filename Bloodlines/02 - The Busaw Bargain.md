# 02 - The Busaw Bargain

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
Before the friars, before the towers,
the Visayas had a hungrier church.

[Verse 1]
The bathilang bathed in the river at night,
called the busaw up from the eaten places —
filth spirits, hunger spirits, hoarders of breath —
and made her price.

[Chorus]
Life and death, life and death,
heal with the left hand and the left hand eats.
Power over leaving, power over staying —
the feast is open, the feast is not free.

[Verse 2]
She walked the sick houses with her shadow behind her,
took the fever into herself and spat it into the mud.
The houses were saved. Something in her
was never saved again.

[Bridge]
Her granddaughter will keep lists
of every fear, filed, in order.
The blood always knew the arithmetic
before the hands did.

[Outro]
In the mud, the thing she spat
opened an eye.
Bathilang, it said, you will die of us —
but never soon enough.
