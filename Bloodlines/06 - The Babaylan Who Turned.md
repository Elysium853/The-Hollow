# 06 - The Babaylan Who Turned

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
The light went to the men with the book.
The dark stayed with the women who watched.

[Verse 1]
I was the babaylan of the good weather,
the one who carried the light in a shell.
Then the light was given a fence,
a lock, a law.

[Chorus]
So I turned. I turned down.
The dalag, the deep-earth serpents,
took my offering of silence
and gave me the root-knot wisdom,
the wrong weather,
the we.

[Verse 2]
We speak in we now —
we under the field, we in the roof,
we the kept women of the turning.
The friars found our carved spirit-houses,
and we set them in the river,
and the river kept their faces.

[Bridge]
Granddaughter, when you mock the dark,
mock it the way we taught: politely,
the way you speak to a cousin
you would not trust with a spoon.

[Outro]
The light built its church over us.
We are the reason the church
has always heard singing
in the floor.
