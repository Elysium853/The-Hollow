# 12 - Four Fires

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
The truck is packed. The ice is bought —
the road is the only road.
The grandmothers stand where thresholds thicken.

[Verse 1]
They will drive into the folded green hills,
four women who have loved each other
half a lifetime — the part the hollow
cannot count.

[Chorus]
Four tents, one fire, we came to heal
what daylight broke —
sing it halfway, daughters.
Finish nothing. Leave one verse
for the one who refuses.

[Verse 2]
The hollow believes it finishes every song.
Let it believe. The verse it cannot finish
holds a granddaughter not yet born,
already refusing.

[Bridge]
We set the table. We paid the price.
We carried the count so far —
to this threshold, this fire, this ordinary truck
on an ordinary road.

[Final Chorus]
Four tents, one fire, we came to heal
what daylight broke —
the blood goes forward,
and the blood says no,
the grandmothers singing behind their voices,
all the way to the red hollow.

[Outro]
The story continues. The song is theirs now.
