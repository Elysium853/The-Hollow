# 09 - The Springs and the Fourth Name

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
1565. The fleet, the fire, the springs.
A name went into the water that year,
and we have carried it ever since.

[Verse 1]
The keepers of the springs kept a spring,
then a hill, then a house, then a name —
the fourth name, the one the binding took,
the one that is not hers to keep.

[Chorus]
Carry the water, carry the name,
carry the door with no key.
The hollow counted four and counted right —
but the grandmothers were counting, too.

[Verse 2]
Spring, churchyard, almshouse, bamboo train,
the cousins of Bicol, the waiting sheds of Manila,
the nurse-ships, the night planes, the rails —
we moved the spring across the water
in a thermos of common water.

[Bridge]
She was born in the town by the railroad,
warm as a loaf, calm as a ledger,
and the spring in her veins sang
the count of four in order.

[Outro]
The fourth name is not hers.
It is ours to have kept, hers to spend —
and when the hollow takes it,
she will be the one who gives it.
