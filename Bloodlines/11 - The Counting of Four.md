# 11 - The Counting of Four

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
Lina, born nineteen-seventy-nine —
Marisol, nineteen-eighty —
Tess, nineteen-eighty-one —
Jo, the keeper of the count's oldest year, nineteen-seventy-eight.
Four children in four hospitals,
none knowing.

[Verse 1]
Lina with her lists, her crisis and order —
the feast in a white coat.
Marisol with the choir pitch —
the trade in a hospital hall.
Tess with the plural we of her jokes —
the turning, sharp, polite.
Jo, calm keeper of four places —
the spring, still warm.

[Chorus]
The grandmothers leaned on kitchen doors,
recognized ourselves.
Four pacts, four hungers, one generation —
one classroom, one city, one friendship,
half a lifetime before fire.

[Verse 2]
They became nurses the way rivers reach the sea,
asked and going.
They loved each other through marriages,
layoffs, funerals, reinventions.
The count held. It always held.

[Bridge]
Let them be ordinary as long as possible.
Let the ordinary be true.
When the fire comes, may it find them
not four victims — four doors.

[Outro]
We are the grandmothers of the count of four.
It is almost time for them to hear us.
