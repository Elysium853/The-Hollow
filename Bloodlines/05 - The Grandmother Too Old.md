# 05 - The Grandmother Too Old

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
They say the mangkukulam's mothers have no graves.
They are simply still home.

[Verse 1]
I taught the daughters every hymn in the novena,
every cadence, every borrowed bell,
so when the shadow sings my songs
they will hear the seam.

[Chorus]
I am the grandmother too old for the feast,
the one the covenant keeps for company.
Sing, daughters — sing the true voice enough
that the borrowed one sounds borrowed.

[Verse 2]
In the year of the Japanese, then the year of the Americans,
I carried the novena into the shelter,
and the shadow carried the shelter,
and the night was a lesson.

[Bridge]
Marisol, when you speak in my cadence,
when your breath finds a harmony —
hold it like a knife in a choir robe:
beautiful, and held.

[Outro]
They gave me centuries, not a life.
I give you a voice that can hear
the difference between a prayer
and a prayer wearing a face.
