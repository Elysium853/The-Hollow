# 10 - The Years of the Long Knives

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
The years of the long knives, and after them
the night planes. The grandmothers survived both,
and the fever they carried survived all.

[Verse 1]
The war came up the hill with its letters;
we went up into the bamboo, the feast under the roots;
nothing we loved was not hungry.

[Verse 2]
Then peace, the nurses' examination,
the linen-luggage, the half-song
folded like a receipt — we crossed
night planes, hands empty, count intact.

[Chorus]
We did not smuggle idols. We smuggled the count.
We did not bring altars. We brought the voices.
Four, four, four, four —
carried in the throat, in the marrow,
in the lullaby under the thermos.

[Bridge]
Manila, Honolulu, San Francisco —
the grandmothers in the kitchens of the new country,
their pots, their prayers, their secret song
with no tune for the new weather.

[Outro]
They think we came with nothing. We came with everything:
the feast, the trade, the turning, the table,
four empty places set across the water.
