# 07 - The Dowry of Deep Earth

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
Every planting, the first fruit went into the furrow.
Every harvest, the first thread went down the same road.

[Verse 1]
The dowry is simple. We owed the deep earth
the first of everything, and it kept
the rice with a spine in it,
the water sweet, the children upright.

[Chorus]
Serpent, serpent, take the first —
and leave the rest to stand.
We are the dowry's women;
we feed you, so you feed us,
and the field remembers whose it is.

[Verse 2]
When the haciendas took the land with papers,
we fed stones to the fields, and the land
learned refusal in its roots —
and the crop came thin on purpose.

[Bridge]
Tess, guarded as a locked granary,
sarcastic as a drought —
you are the field that remembers
whose it is.

[Outro]
The dowry is still owed.
The first of everything.
Even the first word of the story
that is not ours to tell.
