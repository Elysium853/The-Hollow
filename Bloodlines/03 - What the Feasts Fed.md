# 03 - What the Feasts Fed

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
After the banners came, the feast went under the house.
The friars wrote our names wrong on purpose.
We let them.

[Verse 1]
Baptism by the river, saints on the wall,
and under the floor the old mouth still breathing.
We sang the new hymns loudly —
the way you turn a key loudly to hide a lock.

[Chorus]
What the feasts fed is still hungry,
still eating, still Lina's hands before Lina.
Every midwife who took a fever
took it into the line and passed it on.

[Verse 2]
They burned the women with the old medicines.
We stood in the square and watched,
and fixed our eyes on the bell,
and the bell tolled in the count of four.

[Bridge]
Erase the names. Erase the words.
Keep the hands. Keep the hunger.
The child who heals will come — we will know her
by the way she treats fear like a list.

[Outro]
The feast is under the house.
The house is under the hill.
The hill is a door the priests
will never learn to open.
