# 04 - The Mangkukulam's Trade

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
The lowlands, the lamplit barrio, the long years.
She is the one the children are told to be polite to.

[Verse 1]
I made my covenant in the year of the long rains —
shadow for shadow, name for name.
They gave me the years no one counted.
In return I gave the shape of my face.

[Chorus]
I outlived my husband. I outlived my children.
I held the grandchildren of grandchildren.
The barrio forgot to count me —
the way you stop counting a clock.

[Verse 2]
The entities come at the hour of the lamp,
sit in the chair the cat used to sleep in,
ask after the harvest, ask after me,
the way old friends do.

[Bridge]
Granddaughter, choir-child of the hospitals —
when you hear a cadence in a stranger's breath,
it is the trade, singing the song
it made me learn.

[Outro]
They bought my death. I have been
their widow ever since —
long, gentle, feared,
and so, so fond of the girls
who keep the count.
