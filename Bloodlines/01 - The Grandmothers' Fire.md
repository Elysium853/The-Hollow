# 01 - The Grandmothers' Fire

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
Four bloods, one fire. The hollow counted them;
the grandmothers counted the hollow first.

[Verse 1: the Visayan line]
We ate at the busaw feast, healers of the left hand —
we come because Lina comes.

[Verse 2: the Tagalog line]
We traded humanity for the long lamplight —
the barrio's gentle terror; we come because Marisol comes.

[Verse 3: the babaylan line]
We turned from light to the serpents of deep earth —
wise women of the wrong weather; we come because Tess comes.

[Verse 4: the anito line]
We set the table for the dead, drank the sorrow —
keepers of the fourth name; we come because Jo comes.

[Chorus]
Bloodlines, bloodlines, remember the pact —
ten, eleven, twelve, and one to come.
We carried the count through the water
so that one fire could be four.

[Bridge]
Daughters of daughters, we sing
so the count holds. Four. Four. Four.

[Outro]
We are the grandmothers of the coming fire,
singing before the sea
had the name the ships used.
