# 08 - The Table Set for Ghosts

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
In Bicol, the old houses set one extra plate.
The plate is not for the stranger.

[Verse 1]
We set the table for the ones who left,
and the ones who left sat down.
We poured the rice wine dark with our blood —
a thread of us in every glass —
and the dead drank, and were pleased,
and stayed near.

[Chorus]
Keepers of the dead, drinkers of sorrow,
we are the line that warms what was buried.
Every grief has a mouth to feed —
the anito table is never empty.

[Verse 2]
The dead borrow the warm ones' shapes,
the littlest one in the middle,
and ask for the evening song,
and we sing, and we do not look
at how the song ends.

[Bridge]
Jo, gentle as the cup you pour,
widow of the long kindness —
the dead you have fed all your life
are the dead who will carry you down.

[Outro]
Sorrow is a wine, and someone must drink it.
We are the someones.
The table is set four places deep,
waiting for the warm.
