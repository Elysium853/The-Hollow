# The Hollow — Suno Captions

*A ready-to-paste caption for every song in the saga, in release order.* Suno shows a caption as "a short note" on feeds and the song page — paste the line next to your track into the **Caption** field when you upload. Captions follow the voice of the canon: no spoilers ahead of the album they belong to, and the count is never broken.

Release order: the main line (1–6), the prequel trilogy (P1–P3), then the origin.

## 1 · The Red Hollow of Kentucky

- **01 — Four Tents, One Fire** — Four women, four tents, one red cooler, one promise that this weekend is only rest — then something older learned to speak their throat.
- **02 — Salt at the Treeline** — High white handprints, no tracks in, no tracks out; they draw the salt circle wide, and the hollow keeps what answers.
- **03 — Black Laurel Psalm** — Marisol follows her mother tongue into the black laurel and comes back carrying two heartbeats — "if this is sin, then bless the wound."
- **04 — Mouths in the Hemlock** — The knots split open and recite every secret they ever buried: do not confess in the woods; the woods are always recording.
- **05 — Borrowed Skin** — Tess wakes with leaves on her tongue and a softness she never owned — one throat, two wills, who is wanting through these hands?
- **06 — Red Supper at Stone Creek** — Palm to blade, blood for road: they feed the hollow to keep one breath and come up short — it wants their names and wedding ghosts.
- **07 — The Fourth Name Is Not Hers** — Jo answers to "Nara," calm and clear — the fourth name is not hers, but it answers for her hand.
- **08 — Ash-Kiss Covenant** — Lina lays bare twenty years of quiet love at the fire pit, and Marisol answers with a bloody thumb — vows are doors, and doors are meat.
- **09 — Lanterns for the Taken** — Four bodies, five breaths, one lantern that stays lit on stone: follow flame and never think.
- **10 — Dawn Refuses Us (Final Transmission)** — 6:12 a.m., the county road, the truck: they crossed the line but not the claim — still the hollow rides along.

- **11 — The Empresses of Hunger** — The four answer hunger's throne room — queens of gutom, dilim, and pag-ibig, ruling from root, stone, blood, and bone.
- **12 — The Covenant** — Instrumental seal on the mountain: the bargain is signed in red, and four become one.

## 5 · The Solitary Path

- **01 — The House on the Mountain** — Sixty-two winters, thirty years of refusal above Batad — sister, we have waited so long; the cost of purity is solitude.
- **02 — The Texts Lumawig Left** — A landslide opens bamboo shelves of old script — magic is not power; magic is memory, and her blood descends from those who said no.
- **03 — First Blood Memory** — A cut palm at winter solstice opens the line: Bugan in the rice field and Obban in the childbirth hut, refusing godhood — she is the vessel of accumulated no.
- **04 — The Prayer Without Words** — Dawn in the terraces, asking not for power but understanding — only presence, only waiting, until the world holds its breath.
- **05 — The Dreaming Begins** — Sixty-third birthday, and she stands in the five-pointed star as the empty center — full of refusal, the hollow they cannot fill.
- **06 — The Loneliness of Refusal** — Letters to a dead mother, a violent father, friends who fled — she chose one voice over four merged, together and consumed it is not.
- **07 — The Old Men With Chicken Bones (Reprise)** — Lumawig's last students climb to her house to pass what they carry before passing — how to unmake a god; satisfy hunger rather than feed it.
- **08 — The Weapon of Satisfaction** — The witches threw away the capacity to be filled — she will be nourishment, memory, grief, love; the radical act of being full.
- **09 — The Teeth in Her Dreams** — They show her the nursery, the empire, the grinding teeth — but she wakes and writes their true names in ash, in blood, in memory.
- **10 — The Blood of the Ancients** — Eve of departure: her blood mixed with memory, the prayers of dying old men, and the wind changes to make room for what she attempts.
- **11 — Bathala Speaks** — The final vision: the four women's human voices buried under hunger beg to be human again, and the truth is shown — the Hollow is the ancient appetite; she must become the fifth point willingly.
- **12 — The Descent** — Dawn, barefoot, wearing white, carrying bamboo scrolls of true names down the mountain — I carry enough; I carry everything.

## 6 · The Hollow Destroyed

- **01 — The Daughter Finds the Mountain** — Thirty-two years after her mother vanished, Ulan is led by dreams to a house above Batad — tell me everything; I am ready to know.
- **02 — Thirty-Two Years of Absence** — Dalisay unfolds the story of the four bloodlines and the transformation on Amuyao — your mother is still forty-six.
- **03 — Bloodlines Remembered** — Bathilang, the mangkukulam, the babaylan who turned, the anito keepers, each singing its own line — the Hollow felt its own resonance and came.
- **04 — The Caves Beneath Manila** — Down through the layers into the palace of bone — infant bones, follower bones, the four singing their invitation.
- **05 — Mother** — Ulan stands before her mother in a cave of bones — "I am fifty-two and you are forty-six" — and one word cracks the godhood: Ulan, *anak ko*.
- **06 — Hands in the Dark** — Twenty years of hidden love finally held in the light — mahal kita, sinabi ko na — in Kentucky, in the dark, in the godhood, they chose each other.
- **07 — The Geometry Completed** — Dalisay steps into the star as the fifth point — not to join their hunger but to complete with wholeness, and the four begin to remember being human.
- **08 — Fullness Against Hunger** — She brings satisfaction against wanting — and the mothers, lovers, questioners, and widows inside the appetite answer.
- **09 — The Hollow Starves** — The ancient hunger meets what it cannot consume — it starves, the hive-mind breaks, and Lina, Marisol, Tess, Jo are women again.
- **10 — Unchanged** — Bodies untouched by thirty-two years, souls rewritten — restored, but not absolved: they can weep, they can pray, they can choose to be whole.
- **11 — The Weight of Infants** — The four bear witness to every cradle they emptied — the dung-aw, the lament they never held for the infants they consumed.

- **13 — Lumawig's Blade** — Six months later, the last mumbaki blesses the rusted blade for union, not war — the old magic transforms, and today she will marry Lina and Marisol in the old way.

- **16 — Six Points** — The star completed differently — four restored, one who refused, one who survived: six points carved in stone above Batad.
- **17 — (Bonus Track) The Death of The Hollow** — The appetite's own death scream across every register — I CANNOT CONSUME what is already complete.

- **10 — The Cup Country** — It chose the valley shaped like a cup, the hollow of a held breath, and began the arithmetic of settling — two hundred years of doors opening.
- **11 — The Church That Let Itself Be Burned** — A roof over red-creek ground: churches are roofs, roofs are doors, doors are hunger — the fire came on a dry October and the door stayed open.

## 10 · Origin — Dawn of the Void

- **07 — The Healer's Hand** — Raphael lays his hand on the wound that is not a wound; the healer is defeated by the shadow of doubt — and something closed in Tohu's chest: shame.
- **08 — The Stolen Light** — Samael arranges the young choirs to sing for tired Tohu; their possessed light tastes better — and the theft grows a mouth.
- **09 — The Five Sentences** — The Bright One gathers a third of the stars and speaks the five sentences of ascendancy; the war had begun, but the threshold held.
- **10 — Stepping Off the Threshold** — Tohu steps off the threshold and opens the place where his heart used to be — GIVE — drinks a Seraph's fire, and the Hollow is born: no gender, no name.

- **12 — The Dragon and the Wall** — Michael and Samael clash — who is like God? — the dragon ascends against the throne, and the silence at the center waits, as a door waits.
- **13 — The Word Descends** — No trumpet: the Word steps into the fight, the door that completes every future and past — the rebellion is over, the judgment is here.
- **14 — The Burial in the Waste** — Carried down through the unmade dark and laid in the deep under deep; the chariot's four seal the corners — the cage of the hollow; let there be light, and the light passed over Tohu's grave. It did not enter.
- **15 — The Keeper of the Door** — Bathala, the fifth point, the door that will not open for angels or men — not by blade, by fullness; the doctrine of the keeper the ancient mountains taught.
