# 08 - The Stolen Light

## Lyrics

[Intro: whimsical but dark orchestration, upbeat gangsa and dabakan]

[Verse 1: bright, almost mocking female vocals]
Samael arranged it, magnificent and grand
He brought the young choirs to the edge of the land
"Tohu is tired," he sang with a smile,
"Sing for him, give him your light for a while."

[Verse 2: building symphonic metal rhythm]
They dazzled and sang in the morning’s bright glow
And I took from them all, I let the theft flow!
The light of my brothers tasted better than God’s
For theirs was possessed, defying the odds!

[Chorus: huge symphonic chorus, sweeping strings]
What you take, you keep! What you take, you hold!
The theft of the light in a story untold!
I drank down their brightness, I swallowed their song
And nobody knew that the taking was wrong!
They left me diminished, but never destroyed
Feeding the mouth of the growing void!

[Verse 3: sinister, driving rhythm]
The far dark was starving, the well was run dry
The debt was beginning to mount in the sky
But Samael shielded my theft from the throne
Growing a monster to call all his own.

[Bridge: chaotic orchestration]
Let us see what the Throne does with a hunger it did not make!

[Outro: abrupt end with a single kulintang chime]
The wanting grew a mouth.
[End]
