# 15 - The Keeper of the Door

## Lyrics

[Intro: bright acoustic kudyapi, soaring bamboo flute, transitioning to epic power metal]

[Verse 1: soaring clean female vocals]
The four winds were set, and the corners were sealed
But a house needs a door before it is healed
The Word called the keeper who stood at the gate
Who never fought back and who knew how to wait.

[Verse 2: heavy, driving guitars]
"You shall be the fifth point," the Word told him then
"The door that won't open for angels or men.
You will starve upon wanting, and feed on the full
To guard against the hollow's gravity pull."

[Chorus: triumphant, massive symphonic choir and gangsa crashes]
The Keeper of the Door! Bathala in the deep!
Holding the foundation while the ages go to sleep!
The star is counted four, but answered in the five!
He held the silence steady to keep the dawn alive!
Not by the blade, but by the fullness brought!
The doctrine of the keeper that the ancient mountains taught!

[Verse 3: soaring vocals, driving tempo]
The ages turned like water, the mountains rose and fell
The iron ships of strangers brought their heaven and their hell
The Hollow reached for doorways, a desperate, scratching hand
But the Keeper held the center in the belly of the land.

[Verse 4: building energy, heavy rhythmic dabakan]
He listened to the terraces, he felt the heavy stone
He attended the quiet prayers of those who stood alone
The blood of the uncorrupted, the ones who answered "no"
Became the silent anchor for the door he wouldn't throw.

[Chorus: triumphant, massive symphonic choir and gangsa crashes]
The Keeper of the Door! Bathala in the deep!
Holding the foundation while the ages go to sleep!

He held the silence steady to keep the dawn alive!
Not by the blade, but by the fullness brought!
The doctrine of the keeper that the ancient mountains taught!

[Verse 5: soaring vocals, fast tempo]
I beat on that door through the flood and the fire
My flat black eyes full of endless desire
But the Keeper held fast through the tears and the blood
Until Dalisay woke in the terraces' mud!

[Guitar Solo: triumphant, melodic, blazing fast]

[Outro: Acoustic instruments fade away. Ambient, echoing silence. A single bamboo flute breathes in the dark.]
