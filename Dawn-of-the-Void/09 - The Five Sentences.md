# 09 - The Five Sentences

## Lyrics

[Intro: blazing fast twin guitar harmony, relentless double bass]

[Verse 1: high-pitched, aggressive power metal vocals]
At the edge of the eternal where the singing grew weak
The Bright One gathered the rebels to speak
No secrets in heaven, just brightness arranged
A third of the stars, their loyalties changed!

[Verse 2: fast, palm-muted riffing]
He opened his mouth at the edge of the night
And spoke the five sentences challenging light!
The pride of the morning, the venom of God
Preparing to shatter the ground where they trod!

[Chorus: epic, soaring choral vocals, rapid drums]
I will ascend into heaven!
Exalt my throne above the stars!
I will sit upon the mount of the congregation!
Ascend above the highest clouds!
I will be like the Most High! I will be the sky!

[Verse 3: frantic, aggressive rhythm]
The heavens shook wildly, the tearing began
The dragon’s tail sweeping the stars from the span
They fell to the earth that was formless and bare
While I stood shaking, breathing the air!

[Guitar Solo: dual sweeping guitars, fast and melodic]

[Outro: heavy, chugging riffs]
The war had begun.
But the threshold held.
[End]
