# 01 - The Scroll of Batad

## Lyrics

[Intro: haunting bamboo flute and heavy distorted gothic bass]
[Atmospheric kulintang bells chiming]

[Verse 1: whispered operatic female vocals]
Sixty years upon the terraces, the green dawn arrives
A landslide breaks the mountain, where the ancient secret hides
The bamboo shelves of Lumawig, untouched by sun or rain
I trace the old mumbaki script and feel the ancient pain.

[Verse 2: building heavy guitars]
The scrolls speak of the daughters who refused the gods on high
Of Bugan and of Obban who would rather bleed and die
But one last cord turned into dust upon my trembling lap
A canyon carved in history, a trap within a trap.

[Chorus: massive choir, driving power metal drums, gangsa crashes]
This is the dawn of the void!
Before the light was born, before the earth was made!
The four you thought were masters are nothing but the blade!
Look down into the deep, look down into the well
And see the empty vessel where the heaven’s shadow fell!

[Verse 3: driving rhythm, fast riffing]
The dream did not descend to me, it rose from underneath
I felt the hunger vibrating inside my very teeth
The voices of the four went still, choked out by the dark
And I was cast into the age before the first bright spark.

[Bridge: stripped down, bass and kulintang only]
You are the one who refused.
Therefore, you are the one who will know.

[Outro: heavy doom fade out]
The vision takes the sky away.
The light begins to show.
[End]
