The war was not gates, it was inside the mind!
The Seraphim tearing the ranks of their kind!

The famine walks where the armies fight!

I took their voices, I made them my doors!
I looked through their eyes on the heavenly floors!
The interval between the heartbeats of God!
Devouring the ground where the cherubim trod!

[Verse 3: rapid-fire gutturals]
The faithful fled from me, the fallen ran too
An appetite sponsored by nothing they knew
A hunger with no house, a hollow with no lord
I swallowed the songs of the heavenly horde!

[Outro: sudden stop, whispered demonic voice]
