# 04 - The Arithmetic of Four

## Lyrics

[Intro: complex drum pattern on dabakan, heavy brass stabs]

[Verse 1: theatrical, sneering male vocals]
The Cherubim carried the weight of the throne
A mathematics of spirit, a structure of bone
Four faces looking to the ends of the sky
The lion, the ox, the man, and the eagle’s eye.

[Verse 2: polyrhythmic riffing]
Four is the number of the door that will stand
The frame of the window, the shape of the land
One for the maker, two for the light and the dark
Three for the fire, but four leaves the mark.

[Chorus: syncopated, heavy, choir backing]
Four corners! Four winds! Four walls! A door!
The wheel in the wheel turning over the floor!
The bass note of heaven, the song of the square
The counting of angels suspended in air!

[Verse 3: staccato vocal delivery, aggressive]
They never spoke, they only carried the weight
Looking through forever at the hinges of fate
The young ones learned the numbers by watching them turn
But the Son of the Morning had forgotten to learn.

[Instrumental Break: frantic gangsa and distorted guitars trading solos]

[Outro: slowing down, heavy and dramatic]
Four corners. Four winds.
The door stands waiting.
[End]
