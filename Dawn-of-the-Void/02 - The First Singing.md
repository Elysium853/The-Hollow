# 02 - The First Singing

## Lyrics

[Intro: sweeping orchestral strings and blistering guitar sweep picking]

[Verse 1: soaring male vocals]
In the true beginning, the heavens were a song
The Seraphim were burning, where the holy notes belong
Six wings to cover faces, six wings to cover feet
Crying holy, holy, holy, where the light and shadow meet.

[Verse 2: operatic female vocals]
Michael was the silence that kept the song in line
The wall of God, the answer, the unbending, true design
But brightest of them all, the morning’s golden son
Samael, the perfect seal, the beautiful, the one.

[Chorus: massive symphonic metal choir, gangsa strikes]
Light on the left, and the law on the right!
The universe balanced in terrible light!
No earth to inhabit, no ocean to cross
Only the fire, the singing, the loss!
The music of spheres in a heavenly choir
Before the first lie and before the first fire!

[Verse 3: duet, harmonized]
The Principalities and Powers stood in endless, shining rows
But at the very edge of all, where the holy river goes
The threshold waited silently, where creation met the waste
A border of the formless dark that heaven had not graced.

[Guitar Solo: soaring, neoclassical, rapid arpeggios]

[Outro: fading choir, acoustic kudyapi]
The song was perfect.
The song was pure.
[End]
