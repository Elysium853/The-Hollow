# 35 - [Bonus Track] Nara Released

## Lyrics

[Intro - Piano, gentle, prayerful, subing flute enters]

[Verse 1 - Dalisay speaks, mature woman's voice, warm and motherly]
Nara.
Daughter of the Visayas.
Keeper of the hollow
For four hundred years.

You were the first
To be consumed.
You were the first
To sing
To keep it quiet.

[Verse 2 - Nara responds, young girl's voice, healing and innocent]
I was sixteen.
I was afraid.
I sang
Because I did not know
What else to do.

[Verse 3 - Dalisay prays, female voice strong and certain]
I cleanse you
With water
From the terraces.
I cleanse you
With the name
Of Bugan
And Obban.

You are not
The hollow.
You are not
The vessel.
You are Nara.
Only Nara.

[Chorus - Both female voices, harmony, building]
[Dalisay (soprano)]
Ascend, Nara.
[Nara (young voice)]
I ascend.
[Dalisay (alto)]
The heavens wait.
[Nara (clear)]
The hollow
Is no longer
My home.

[Bridge - Women's choir enters, all female voices, triumphant]
Lumawig receives you.
The ancestors
Welcome you.
The four hundred years
Are washed away.

[Nara (young female)]
I am clean.
I am free.
I am sixteen
And sixty
And released.

[Final Chorus - Full female orchestration, transcendent]
Nara ascends!
The first keeper
Is the first
Released!

[Dalisay (motherly female)]
Go in peace.
[Nara (young female)]
I go in peace.
[Dalisay (warm alto)]
The door is open.
[Nara (clear soprano)]
I am
Finally
Home.

[Outro - Piano and subing, fading into light, two female voices humming harmony]
[End]
