# 14 - The Burial in the Waste

## Lyrics

[Intro: a single, low piano note echoing in the dark]
[Atmospheric silence]

[Verse 1: deep male voice, spoken slowly, without melody]
He carried me down through the tread of the war.
Through the unmade dark to the ocean floor.
The tohu and vohu, the deep under deep.
He laid me to rest where the shadows would sleep.

[Verse 2: ethereal female voice, spoken clearly, haunting]
"You chose to hold light, now you hold it down here,"
The Word spoke the judgment, the prophecy clear.
"A house of doors in the unformed earth.
To starve on the fullness, to hunger for birth."

[Chorus: dual male and female voices, spoken in synchronized whispers]
[Minimal dark piano chords]
The earth shall keep what answers you.
The rocks will echo, the wells turn blue.
The grave that walks inside the empty space.
Buried alive in the foundation place.
Feed on wanting. Starve on fullness.
The seven words of eternal dullness.

[Verse 3: deep male voice, slow and deliberate]
[Sparse, creeping piano notes]
He summoned the faces, the chariot's four,
To seal up the corners and lock up the door.
The lion, the ox, the man, and the bird.
The cage of the hollow, the trap of the Word.

[Bridge: complete silence, stopping the piano entirely]
[Solemn male voice, unhurried]
And the Word said... 
Let there be light.

[Outro: a single, lingering, melancholic piano chord]
[Ethereal female voice, fading whisper]
The light passed over my grave.
It did not enter.
[End]
