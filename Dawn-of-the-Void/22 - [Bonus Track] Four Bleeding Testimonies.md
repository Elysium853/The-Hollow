# 22 - [Bonus Track] Four Bleeding Testimonies

## Lyrics

**Featuring:** **Lina**, **Marisol**, **Tess**, **Jo**

**[Intro - Solo piano, gentle, dark, minor key, Debussy-esque but simpler, single notes ringing]**

---

**[Voice 1: LINA - Age 34, lower register, smoker's rasp, Filipino accent, weary but unbreakable, speaks slowly, each word weighted]**

*Lina:*
I am the first.
The eldest daughter of the eldest daughter.
My grandmother's grandmother
Bled into the rice terraces
And the earth drank her
But did not give her back.

I speak from the wound
That never learned to close.
My testimony is this:
I remember the Hollow
Before I knew its name.
In dreams, it called me
*Tohu.*
As if I were the vessel
And not the voice
That refuses
To be
Filled.

---

**[Piano continues, same motif, slightly more notes added, gentle decay]**

---

**[Voice 2: MARISOL - Age 29, middle register, trembling vibrato, tears in throat, breathy, emotional breaks between phrases, builds to strength by end]**

*Marisol:*
I am the second.
The one who tried
To pray it away.
Who knelt in churches
While the Hollow knelt
Inside her.

My testimony bleeds
From the place
Where faith
Used to live.
I thought I was empty.
I thought that was
The sin.
But the emptiness
Was just
*Room.*
Room for four voices.
Room for the blood
To finally
Speak.

---

**[Piano shifts, lower register, single bass notes, ominous but gentle, space between notes]**

---

**[Voice 3: TESS - Age 24, higher register, clear and bell-like but knowing, young woman's voice with ancient weight, precise diction, almost clinical detachment that cracks into feeling, faster speech pattern]**

*Tess:*
I am the third.
The one who read the books.
Who found the Scroll of Batad
In my mother's
Unmarked box.

My testimony
Is written in margins.
In the space
Between what history
Records
And what blood
Remembers.
I know the arithmetic:
Four faces of the Cherubim.
Four winds.
Four corners.
And the fifth—
*The door.*
We are not the door.
We are the ones
Who finally
*Close it.*

---

**[Piano builds, two hands now, gentle arpeggios, still dark, no resolution, hanging chords]**

---

**[Voice 4: JO - Age 19, raw, untrained, fierce, hoarse from screaming, working class accent, speaks fast then slows, youngest but most certain, voice closest to breaking but never breaks]**

*Jo:*
I am the fourth.
The one they didn't
Think would survive.
The miscarriage
That lived.
The bleeding
That wouldn't stop
Until it became
*Language.*

My testimony
Is the shortest:
Fuck the Hollow.
Fuck its hunger.
Fuck its lullaby.
I am the wound
That learned
To scream
In four-part
Harmony.

---

**[Piano pauses, single note holds, silence for 3 seconds]**

---

**[ALL FOUR VOICES TOGETHER - Lina low and steady, Marisol trembling on top, Tess precise and clear, Jo raw and fierce, overlapping slightly, creating chord of voices, no unison, four distinct pitches]**

*Lina:* We are the bleeding.
*Marisol:* We are the testimony.
*Tess:* We are the arithmetic completed.
*Jo:* We are the door closing.

*Together:*
*Four wounds.*
*Four mouths.*
*One word.*
*And the word is*
*ENOUGH.*

---

**[Piano returns, final chord, unresolved, ringing out, slow decay into silence]**
[End]
