# 07 - The Healer's Hand

## Lyrics

[Intro: slow, crushing doom riffs, mournful cello]

[Verse 1: deep, slow operatic vocals]
Raphael found me, the healer of wings
When the song came through diminished in strings
"Brother," he said, "something closed in your chest."
He laid down his hand where my brightness should rest.

[Verse 2: heavy, dragging rhythm]
The hand that could close any wound in the sky
Met the first broken door that refused to comply
It was not a break, it was a choice I had made
The healer’s great power began to degrade.

[Chorus: massive, echoing gothic choir]
What are you keeping? What do you hide?
The vessel of heaven is rotting inside!
The power pours in, and the power pours out
The healer is defeated by the shadow of doubt!
No wound to be stitched, no bone to repair
Just a door held shut in the heavenly air!

[Verse 3: spoken word over heavy bass]
Uriel said to cut the wound out with fire.
But Michael, the wall, the silent defier,
Said I was loved, not a tool to be broken.
But the words of the morning had already been spoken.

[Outro: slow fade with cello and bass]
Shame. I felt shame.
I hid behind the Bright One.
[End]
