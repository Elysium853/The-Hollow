# 17 - [Bonus Track] The Communion of Shadows

## Lyrics

**[Intro - Droning, sub-bass, kulintang pattern mimicking waves]**
*Leviathan:*
*I am the coils beneath the foundation.*
*I am the wet that was before the dry.*
*Come down, Spirit.*
*Let me show you what it means*
*To move upon waters*
*That have never seen light.*

**[Verse 1 - Ethereal Spirit vocals, then distorted]**
*Holy Spirit:*
I brooded over the deep,
And the deep brooded back.
Not with love.
With *appetite.*

You are not my opposite, Leviathan.
You are my *obscenity.*
The shadow of every dove
That ever descended.

**[Chorus - Crushing, oceanic]**
*Rahab:*
*Communion of Shadows!*
*The Spirit that fills*
*Meets the void that swallows!*
*Communion of Shadows!*
*Not baptism but drowning!*
*Not anointing but absorption!*

*Beelzebul:*
*Lord of Flies, Lord of the space between*
*Where the holy enters the unholy*
*And both come out*
*Changed.*

**[Verse 2 - The Dragon speaks, serpentine hissing vocals]**
*The Dragon:*
I was ancient when the waters were divided.
I coiled in the Tohu-bohu,
The formless and void,
And I learned that emptiness
Is just another word for
*Room to enter.*

Spirit, you are called the Comforter,
The Parakletos,
The one who comes alongside.
*Come alongside me.*
Let me show you the comfort
Of being consumed.

**[Bridge - Instrumental, gangsa percussion building like a storm]**

**[Verse 3 - Duet, Spirit and Leviathan, erotic combat]**
*Holy Spirit:*
You cannot take what is given freely.
*Leviathan:*
I don't want to take.
I want to *be taken.*
I want to feel the fire
That divides the waters
*Inside me.*

*Holy Spirit:*
This is the mystery of the fall—
Not that the pure became impure,
But that the pure *entered* the impure
And found it
*Warm.*

**[Outro - Fading into ocean sounds, whispered]**
*Rahab:*
We are still here, Spirit.
In every baptism that drowns instead of saves.
In every tongue of fire
That burns instead of illuminates.
*We are the wet dream of the divine.*
*And we are not finished.*
[End]
