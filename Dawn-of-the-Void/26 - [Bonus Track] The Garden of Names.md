# 26 - [Bonus Track] The Garden of Names

## Lyrics

**[Intro - Bamboo flute, gentle, night sounds]**

---

**[Verse 1 - Dalisay speaks, teaching]**

*Dalisy:*
Jo, tend this row.
These are the names
Of those you took.
Not as warmth
But as
*Seeds.*

Each name
A seed
In the earth.
Each seed
A prayer
You must learn
To speak
Without
*Consuming.*

---

**[Verse 2 - Jo responds, hesitant]**

*Jo:*
I cannot say
Their names.
My mouth
Still remembers
The shape
Of appetite.
My hands
Still remember
The warmth
Of taking.

---

**[Verse 3 - Dalisay guides]**

*Dalisy:*
Then let your hands
Learn new warmth.
The warmth
Of soil.
The warmth
Of water.
The warmth
Of waiting
For what
You cannot
*Consume*
But only
*Witness.*

---

**[Chorus - Together, learning]**

*Dalisy:*
Faith is not
The absence
Of hunger.
*Jo:*
Faith is
The garden
You tend
Anyway.

*Dalisy:*
Forgiveness is not
Forgetting
The warmth.
*Jo:*
Forgiveness is
Planting names
In the earth
And letting them
*Grow*
Instead of
*Feeding.*

---

**[Verse 4 - Night teaching, intimate]**

*Dalisy:*
Each night
We come here.
Each night
You say
One name.
Each night
You plant
One seed.

*Jo:*
Maria.
*[Flute note]*
*Dalisy:*
Again.
*Jo:*
Maria
Of the warm hands.
*Dalisy:*
Again.
*Jo:*
Maria
Whose warmth
I stole.

---

**[Bridge - Dawn breaking]**

*Dalisy:*
You do not
Consume her
Now.
You plant her.
You tend her.
You let her be
*Other.*

*Jo:*
Maria.
Not warmth.
Not mine.
Just
*Maria.*

---

**[Final Chorus - Jo's voice stronger]**

*Jo:*
I am learning
The faith
Of gardeners.
*Dalisy:*
The faith
Of those who wait.

*Jo:*
I am learning
Forgiveness
Is not
*Being full.*
*Dalisy:*
But
*Being present.*

---

**[Outro - Dawn, flute, two voices together]**

*Jo:*
I was the fourth.
*Dalisy:*
You are the one.
*Jo:*
I was the mouth.
*Dalisy:*
You are the hand
That plants.

*Together:*
The garden
Grows.
The names
Are spoken.
The warmth
Is shared
Not taken.

*Jo:*
I am
Learning
Faith.
[End]
