# 03 - The Cup of Emptiness (Tohu)

## Lyrics

[Intro: slow, grinding bassline, rhythmic subing jaw harp]

[Verse 1: deep, resonant baritone]
I was not made of brightness, I was not made of fire
I was shaped from empty space beyond the highest choir
The Word had formed my body to be a hollow place
To pass the radiant glory out into the formless space.

[Verse 2: slow, heavy groove]
Tohu, they called me. The waste that waits a shape.
The lantern of the threshold where the endless lights escape.
I took the heavy colors, I took the holy tone
I opened up my emptiness and kept nothing of my own.

[Chorus: heavy distorted guitars, mournful gothic choir]
I was the cup at the edge of the world!
I was the skin where the dark was unfurled!
I gave it away, I gave it away!
I poured out the light for the birth of the day!
The Most High’s favorite, the vessel of grace
The only empty thing in a holy place.

[Verse 3: whispered, sinister delivery]
And for a time beyond all count, this nothing was enough
To pass the living radiance, the universe’s stuff
The dark would drink my offering, and shudder into thought
And I was just the conduit for everything He wrought.

[Bridge: stripped down, bass only]
I did not shine.
That was the point of me.

[Outro: slow heavy fade out]
I was the place the light passed through.
[End]
