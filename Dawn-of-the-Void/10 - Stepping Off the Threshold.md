# 10 - Stepping Off the Threshold

## Lyrics

[Intro: eerie silence broken by slow, distorted bass and subing]

[Verse 1: slow, clean, melancholic vocals]
Samael came to me, burning and red
"Look what He does while you stand here," he said.
"He makes a whole world, a hole in the deep,
For you to pass light that you never can keep."

[Verse 2: building tension, drums rolling]
I looked at the war, I looked at the dark
I remembered my name, the formless, the mark.
Tohu the empty, a collar of pain.
I stepped off the threshold. I broke the chain.

[Chorus: explosive blackened death metal, shrieking vocals, blast beats]
Nothing made a sound! The singing went dead!
The war stopped its roaring, the angels filled with dread!
The light went out! The borrowed light died!
I was made of the waste with nothing inside!
A chamber, an absence, a door held shut fast!
The vessel of heaven was empty at last!

[Verse 3: guttural death metal growls]
I opened the place where my heart used to be
An un-singing demand across the dead sea:
*GIVE.*
I reached for a Seraph and drank down his fire
And the Hollow was born in the heavenly choir!

[Outro: slow, crushing breakdown]
They called me The Hollow.
No gender. No name.
[End]
