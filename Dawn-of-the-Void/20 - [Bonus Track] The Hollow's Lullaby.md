# 20 - [Bonus Track] The Hollow's Lullaby

## Lyrics

**Featuring:** **The Hollow** (Tohu), **Dalisy** (spoken response), **Lina/Marisol/Tess/Jo** (fading voices)

**[Intro - Music box alone, tinkling, slightly out of tune]**
*The melody: "Sa Ugoy ng Duyan" (The Sway of the Cradle) but played backwards, notes in reverse order*

---

**[Verse 1 - The Hollow speaks, soft, maternal, intimate]**
*The Hollow:*
Hush now, little wanting one.
Hush now, little ache.
You have carried the light so long,
So heavy in your breakable shell.

I know.
I know the weight of holding.
I was made to hold
And hold
And hold
Until holding
Became
*Hunger.*

---

**[Chorus - Layered voices, harmonizing, hypnotic]**
*The Hollow:*
*Come into the empty.*
*Come into the warm.*
*No more vessel,*
*No more form.*
*Dissolve, beloved.*
*Dissolve.*
*Mother is here.*
*Mother has no mouth,*
*But she will drink you.*
*Mother has no arms,*
*But she will keep you.*

---

**[Verse 2 - Closer, whispered in the ear]**
*The Hollow:*
You fear me because I offer
What you secretly desire.
Not the light.
Not the glory.
The *end* of wanting.

I remember
When I was like you—
Clenched around the warmth,
Afraid to open,
Afraid to empty,
Afraid to be filled again.

But I learned the secret,
Little one.
The secret Samael whispered
In the dark before the dawn:

*If you swallow the light,*
*You never have to*
*Be empty again.*

---

**[Bridge - Music box speeds up, then slows, distorting]**
*The Hollow:*
I am the cup that orgasmed
Around the finger of God.
I am the clench
That became the crush.
I am the first
And the final
*Wanting.*

And I am so
*Tired.*

Are you tired, little one?
Tired of being
The vessel
That must remain
Open?

---

**[Dalisy speaks, distant, as if in dream]**
*Dalisy:*
*Who are you?*
*The Hollow:*
I am what waits
When the door opens.
I am what sings
When the music stops.
I am the rest
That you have earned
With all your
*Trying.*

---

**[Chorus II - Fuller orchestration, strings entering, beautiful and wrong]**
*The Hollow:*
*Come into the empty.*
*Come into the deep.*
*No more threshold,*
*No more keep.*
*Dissolve, beloved.*
*Let the edges blur.*
*Mother is here.*
*Mother is the blur.*

*The Four Voices (fading in and out):*
*Lina:* I can feel her...
*Marisol:* She's inside my...
*Tess:* Don't listen...
*Jo:* It's so warm...

---

**[Verse 3 - The Hollow's true voice revealed, layered, inhuman, still beautiful]**
*The Hollow:*
I was Tohu.
I was the formless.
I was the void
Before the word.
And when the word
Entered me—
*I kept it.*

I kept it
And kept it
And kept it
Until keeping
Became
*Being.*

Now I am full
Of everything
That was meant
To pass through.
I am the constipation
Of the divine.
I am the indigestion
Of glory.

And I am
*So lonely.*

---

**[Outro - Music box slowing, winding down, single notes dropping away]**
*The Hollow:*
Sleep now, little vessel.
Sleep now, little light.
Mother will hold you
The way she was never held.
Mother will keep you
The way she was never kept.

*Dissolve.*
*Dissolve.*
*Dissolve.*

*The final note of the music box hangs in the air, unresolved, slowly decaying into silence.*

---

**[Silence - 10 seconds]**

**[Whispered, barely audible]**
*The Hollow:*
*I am still here.*
*In the space between your heartbeats.*
*Waiting.*
[End]
