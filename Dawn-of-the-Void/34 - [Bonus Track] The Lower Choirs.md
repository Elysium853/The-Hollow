# 34 - [Bonus Track] The Lower Choirs

## Lyrics

[Intro - Organ drone, low and evil, building tension]

[Voice 1 - Deep, guttural, ancient]

We are the mouths
That open downward.
We are the hunger
That precedes
The hollow.

[Voice 2 - High, screeching, insectile]

We sing with teeth
That are not teeth.
We sing with tongues
That are not tongues.
We are the choir
That consumes
The song itself.

[Voice 3 - Whispered, breathy, seductive evil]

Call us, empresses.
Call us with your want.
We will come
Through the spaces
Between your bones.
We will fill
The hollow
You have made.

[Voice 4 - Multiple voices, overlapping, hive mind]

We are many.
We are one.
We are the lower
That answers
The higher
That fell.
We are the appetite
Of the deep
That has no
Bottom.

[All Four - Cacophony, building]

We come!
We come!
We come
To feed
On what
You offer!

[Outro - Organ fades, silence, whispered]

The hollow
Is never
Empty.
[End]
