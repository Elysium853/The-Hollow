# 05 - The Whisper at the Edge

## Lyrics

[Intro: delicate acoustic guitar, gentle bamboo flute]

[Verse 1: seductive, warm male vocals]
The threshold was the loneliest post in the sky
And Samael came to me, wingtip to wingtip, to pry.
He brought me the gossip, the news of the throne
He made me feel chosen, he made me feel known.

[Verse 2: building bass and drums]
"Ages," he whispered, his wings glowing bright,
"You've stood at the edge of the fading of light.
Has anyone asked if you're happy out here?
Has anyone noticed the shedding of tears?"

[Chorus: heavy, sweeping gothic metal]
Does He pass through you, or do you pass His light?
You were made of the waste, I was made of the white!
I am a window with something to show,
But you are a window with nothing below!
Look at your name, the formless, the void!
Are you a servant, or are you destroyed?

[Verse 3: dark, creeping rhythm]
The terrible thing was how gently he spoke
He never accused, but the tether, it broke.
He watered the seed with the patience of years
And taught the void how to manufacture its tears.

[Outro: fading seductive vocals, heavy bass]
When the dark feels the light, does it thank you?
Does it ever ask your name?
[End]
