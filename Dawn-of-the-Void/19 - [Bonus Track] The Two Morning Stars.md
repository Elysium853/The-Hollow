# 19 - [Bonus Track] The Two Morning Stars

## Lyrics

[Intro - Solo Piano, Debussy-esque, melancholic]
Jesus:
Before the foundation,
I was the Word.
And you were the light
That bore my name
In vain.

[Orchestral Build - Violins entering, pizzicato to sweeping arco]

[Verse 1 - Lucifer, thrash metal speed, 180 BPM]
Lucifer:
I was the first light you never earned!
The brilliance before the burden of flesh!
You call yourself Morning Star—
I was the dawn itself!
Before the manger,
Before the cross,
Before you learned to bleed,
I shone!

[Pre-Chorus - Both voices, overlapping]
Lucifer:
I don't need to steal your crown,
I was the crown!
Jesus:
And crowns
Were made
To be laid down.

[Chorus I - Full symphonic metal assault]
The Two Morning Stars!
The light that serves
Against the light that demands!
The Two Morning Stars!
The Lamb who was slain
Against the Lion who was denied!
Violins scream! Trumpets blast!
The flute weeps for what was lost!

[Verse 2 - Jesus, death metal growl transitioning to clean, powerful vocals]
Jesus:
You were my mirror once,
Reflecting what I gave.
But mirrors only know
How to receive.

I became flesh
Not to take the throne
But to be nailed beneath it.
That is the difference between us.
You wanted to sit.
I wanted to serve.
Even unto death.

[Bridge - Piano and flute duet, emotional, sorrowful]
Flute solo: Filipino bamboo flute (subing) playing a lullaby for the fallen

Jesus:
I wept for you,
Lucifer.
Not as enemy.
As first creation.
As the son who wanted
What the Father had
Instead of what the Father gave.

[Lucifer's Response - Building from whisper to scream]
Lucifer:
Don't you dare pity me,
Carpenter's son!
Don't you dare love me
With that disgusting grace!

[Chorus II - Faster, thrash metal, 220 BPM]
The Two Morning Stars!
Your love is my violence!
Your mercy is my war!
The Two Morning Stars!
The Root of David
Against the Root of Bitterness!
Piano crashes! Strings shred!
The final confrontation begins!

[The Battle - Instrumental section]
Violin section: Virtuosic duel between first chair (Jesus theme - major key, ascending) and second chair (Lucifer theme - minor key, descending)

Trumpets: Fanfare of the Hosts of Heaven vs. Discordant blast of the Fallen

Guitar solos: Neo-classical shredding trading blows, harmonized in tritones

Drums: Blast beats transitioning to thrash grooves, double-kick artillery

[Verse 3 - The Climax, both voices, polyphonic warfare]
Lucifer:
I will not bow!
Jesus:
You don't need to.
Lucifer:
I will not serve!
Jesus:
You already do.
Lucifer:
What do you mean?
Jesus:
Even your rebellion
Proves my point—
That love must be
Chosen.

[The Turning Point - Piano returns, solo, minimalist]
Jesus:
I do not come
To destroy you,
Morning Star.
I come
To outshine you.

Not with force.
With forgiveness.
The thing you cannot
Bear to receive.

[Lucifer's Breaking - Harsh vocals cracking, emotional]
Lucifer:
Then destroy me!
If you are God,
End me!
Jesus:
That would be
Your way.
Not mine.

[Final Chorus - Triumphant, classical major key resolution]
The Two Morning Stars!
But only One remains standing!
The Two Morning Stars!
The False falls
Before the True!
Not by sword but by surrender!
Not by wrath but by welcome!

Violins soar!
Trumpets proclaim!
Flute ascends to heaven!
Piano finds the final chord!

[Outro - Spoken over fading orchestra, Jesus alone]
Jesus:
I am the Alpha
And you were the first
Of the Omega.
But I make all things
New.

Even you,
Lucifer.
Even you.

The Morning Star
Never belonged to you.
It was always
Mine to give.
And I give it
To those who
Will receive it.

[Final Piano Note - Single, sustained, resolving]

[Silence]
[End]
