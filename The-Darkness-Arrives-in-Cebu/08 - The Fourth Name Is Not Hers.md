# 08 - The Fourth Name Is Not Hers

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
July 1565. Tupas kneels on his own ground.
The fourth binding of the old covenant:
one blood, one fire, one child found, one king's word.

[Verse 1]
The datu with the burned eyes came down from the hill,
knelt in the shade and swore the peace,
and the general wrote his name in the register.

[Chorus]
One for the blood that sealed the compact,
one for the fire that cleaned the ground,
one for the child the flame refused —
and the fourth name is not hers, and it is not found.
When the fourth name is spoken fully, the hollow thing is no longer blind.

[Verse 2]
The keeper gave her name at the river,
but a covenant of four cannot be finished with one opened door.

[Bridge]
The old women talked to the springs:
"A kneeling man has given it instead."

[Final Chorus]
Tupas stood. The general smiled. The bells rang out —
four doors open, four floors deep;
it will wake for four women and one fire, and it will keep.

[Outro]
[Spoken]
The mountain always counts in four.
