# 07 - Nara the Keeper

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
Her name was Nara, daughter of the babaylan,
keeper of the child in the box for forty-four years.

[Verse 1]
I was fourteen when the queen gave me the child,
I carried it up where the springs are born,
it wanted the names of our beautiful dead,
and I gave them, one by one, by year.

[Chorus]
I am Nara, the keeper, I am the door,
I held the hollow thing for forty-four years —
now the white men have drunk my people's blood at the sandugo;
take this name that will carry it out of the land.

[Verse 2]
It asked, in my mother's voice, for my name; I said no, and it waited —
it has the patience of wood and bone.

[Bridge]
A name is only given when the keeper is done with the keeping.
Let my people bury their dead, and I will pay the last of the toll.

[Outro]
[Spoken]
She gave it her name that night so no other would be taken,
walked into the river at dawn; the friars found no body.
From that day her keepers crossed themselves with the left hand, slowly, like a door.
