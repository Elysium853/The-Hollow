# 11 - Tornaviaje (The Sunrising Road)

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
October 1565. The San Pedro sails east against the sun —
the tornaviaje, the way home the navigator swore existed.

[Verse 1]
We follow the sun the wrong way home,
and the third night out, in the dead watch's lull,
the hold ticks once, like a child's soft chin,
and the watch below swears the crate is full.

[Chorus]
East against the sun, home against the tide,
we sail the road that runs the wrong way —
O Salve Regina, hear us now,
the sea has taken our names for toll;
answer us with any voice but the voice in the hold.

[Verse 2]
Storms came up from the north; we rationed water and wine,
and a man in fever sang a song he never knew.

[Bridge]
At midnight the whole crew heard the Salve come back —
a child's voice in the rigging, note for note, exact.

[Outro]
[Spoken — the navigator's log, Pacific, November]
November 8. The cargo is singing in a woman's voice.
I have charted the road home — not what we brought back on it.
