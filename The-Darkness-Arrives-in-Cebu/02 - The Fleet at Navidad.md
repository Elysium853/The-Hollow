# 02 - The Fleet at Navidad

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
Puerto de la Navidad, November 1564 —
they call it an expedition; the ships call it a debt.

[Verse 1]
Five hulls at Navidad, rigged and tarred,
the general reads the royal decree —
the monks trace crosses on the tide,
and something under the harbor mud
has begun to hold its breath and follow.

[Chorus]
Set the sail and let the canvas tear,
we sail to claim the years not yet begun —
iron, incense, and a king's despair,
five ships going out like monks at noon,
and no one thinks to ask the deep what it expects from us in return.

[Verse 2]
The oldest monk keeps a chart in his head —
a road that runs east when all roads begin;
at night the sea knocks once, and knocks like itself.

[Bridge]
They sing the old chanty; a fifth voice rides —
the wind, this night, has the king's eyes.

[Outro]
[Spoken]
On the eve of departure the general murmured a name not his own,
taught to him, he said, by a sea that had a child's voice.
