# 03 - One Blood (The Sandugo)

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
Bohol, mid-March 1565 — two men drink a covenant
into the only vessel that can hold it: blood.

[Verse 1 - Legazpi]
I sailed the wine-dark sea to plant a cross and leave a stake —
give me your wood, your port, your peace; the crown keeps you safe.

[Chorus - Both]
One blood in the cup, one blood in the ground,
we are brothers from the palm to the hand —
one blood, one vow, sealed in wine we pour.

[Verse 2 - Sikatuna]
There is a mountain that remembers four;
it taught the dark the shape of blood —
and I have waited all my years to give it a captain, a flood.

[Bridge - Sikatuna, low]
Take our hunger, captain, take it aboard —
the salt water keeps what it is given — clean.

[Final Chorus - Layered]
The general drinks; in the hills the keeper cuts her palm:
"Be brothers now, child. One blood. One flood."
And the child, for the first time in forty-four years, smiles back.

[Outro - Sikatuna, to his son]
We have given them what was waiting. May the salt water keep it.
