# 01 - Forty-Four Years

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
She was fourteen when the ships came;
a wooden child put in her hands.
It was never theirs to give.

[Verse 1]
Three hulls up from the southern sea,
white sails from a bone that dried,
the queen held the child, and it opened its eyes,
and watched me carry it home.

[Chorus]
Forty-four years I fed it the names,
forty-four harvests I cut my palm,
it asked for a lullaby, never the end,
it asked for my mother, and I gave it her song.
It was never theirs to give, and now it is mine to keep.

[Verse 2]
The short king died on the neighboring sand,
we feasted the iron men one last good night,
and the queen came wild: "Take it, keeper —
keep it from the fire, keep it from the priests."

[Bridge]
A name is a door, and the dead don't mind,
and something behind it is listening through.

[Outro]
[Whispered]
One day something will ask for my name —
and I will give it. I am tired of being the only door.
