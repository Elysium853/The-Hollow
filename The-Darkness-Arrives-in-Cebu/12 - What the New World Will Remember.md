# 12 - What the New World Will Remember

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
Acapulco, November 8, 1565.
One stevedore will say, for his whole life, the crate had a heartbeat.

[Verse 1]
The road from the west is found at last,
but the boy behind the cart counts his shadow twice.

[Chorus]
Four hundred years it will take to know it,
four centuries to cross a green land's spine —
the hollow thing has crossed the widest water
and found a continent to cross the same way.

[Verse 2]
It will ride the mule trains, hang in mission adobe,
and rest in a red-hollow valley, waiting for a fire and a name.

[Bridge]
They will build a church where the water sings and burn it down —
but the mountain remembers; it counts in four.

[Final Chorus]
Four tents, one fire, on a hollow-ground night —
the darkness did not arrive in Cebu to end there.
It arrived in Cebu to get here.

[Outro]
[Spoken — the sexton, four hundred years later, muffled]
The child is still awake, learning,
one hollow at a time, how to get home.
