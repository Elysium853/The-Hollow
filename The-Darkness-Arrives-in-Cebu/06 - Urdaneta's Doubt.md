# 06 - Urdaneta's Doubt

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
Fray Andrés de Urdaneta, navigator, has never once doubted a star.
In the candlelight of a burned-out church, he doubts the child.

[Verse 1]
I have sailed where the sea has no bottom,
I have prayed in the wind that has no name,
but I have not sailed a stranger water
than the smile on that painted face.

[Chorus]
Forgive me, God, for I have doubted
the miracle of a warm wooden child —
the box the fire would not swallow,
the eyes that watch and are not filed.
I was sent to be the navigator, and I have lost the wind a while.

[Verse 2]
The keeper came down from the hill country:
"It was never theirs. It was always being found.
The question is what will find it when you are gone."

[Bridge]
I write my log, I cross it out,
I write it small where the sea can't read:
cargo takes on the shape of the hold.

[Outro]
[Spoken — low, to the candle]
Tonight the child keeps its own vigil;
one of us is praying.
The candle will not tell me which.
