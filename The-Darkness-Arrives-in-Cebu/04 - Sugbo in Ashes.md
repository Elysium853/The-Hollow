# 04 - Sugbo in Ashes

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
April 27, 1565. Six hours of cannonade,
then the landing boats. No feast this time. No trick.

[Verse 1]
They came with the dawn and the smoke of the canon,
they came with torches and singing priests,
Sugbo went up like a dry palm begging,
the harbor turned red as a newborn's wrists.

[Chorus]
Sugbo in ashes, Sugbo in smoke —
they have taken our harbor, our houses, our names,
but the box still waits on its altar stone,
and the child in the box is still not wood,
and a hunger fed for forty-four years will not feed on iron and blood.

[Verse 2]
Tupas watched from the hill's high grass,
fists full of ash — "I will kneel, captain,
the day that I'm dead."

[Bridge]
At midnight the salt rain came,
and a girl found the tindalo box untouched by flame —
fire refuses what is already hollow inside.

[Outro]
They think they burned us. They do not know
what they found on the smoke's sixth day.
