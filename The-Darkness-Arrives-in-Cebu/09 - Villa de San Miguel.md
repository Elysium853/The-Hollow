# 09 - Villa de San Miguel

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
May 1565. Villa de San Miguel, the first permanent settlement,
built on the burned ground of Sugbo.

[Verse 1]
We hammer the walls on the ash of the houses,
we raise the roof on the bones of the dead —
the masons joke the ground gives a foundation for free,
and the well comes up dark though the rain came long.

[Chorus]
Bang the anvil, raise the villa,
name the streets for the saints you know —
we are building a claim that can't be taken,
but the ground was never empty:
we are building a box, and the box has a floor.

[Verse 2]
The bells are cast from the captured culverins,
the bronze that killed us now sings for the feast;
the old men hear the peal come back three times cold.

[Bridge]
The whole town filed past to kiss the glass case,
while the old women of the burned water watched the child for a first, slow turn.

[Outro]
[Spoken — a sexton, years later]
They ask why the choir always sounds like one voice too many.
The child knows where it lives.
