# 05 - The Child in the Box

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
The village burned two days; the rain soaked the third.
Juan de Camus hunted the ash and found a hut the fire refused — and in the box, a child.

[Verse 1]
One wall stood where the fire had turned;
Camus pried the lid with his cutlass tip,
and the child looked up from its folded cloth
with flat black eyes and a painted lip.

[Chorus]
A child in a box in a burned-down house,
warm to the touch though the roof fell in —
miracle, miracle, the sailors shout,
kneel in the ash of the ruin:
a child in a box, and the box is a drum.

[Verse 2]
Word went out; the soldiers knelt in the ash and rain,
the general came down to look,
and the child watched all of them, small and vain.

[Bridge]
But the old monk stood apart in the rain:
"A child was given to the queen, forty-four years ago.
If this is the same child, then something kept it well."

[Outro]
[Spoken — the old monk, to no one]
And no one asks what I am asking:
if fire would not take him — what is he made of?
