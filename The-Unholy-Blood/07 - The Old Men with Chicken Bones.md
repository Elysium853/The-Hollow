# 07 - The Old Men with Chicken Bones

## Lyrics

**[Intro: Field recording of actual *baki* ceremony]**
*(Sound of chicken bones being cast, rustling in dirt, the murmur of old men speaking Ifugao)*

**[Verse 1: The Mumbaki Descent]**

Lumawig walks with rusted blade
Eighty years of speaking to the stone
From Banaue to Batad, the call went out
Seven priests, twenty more, the old ways mobilized

We carry *dawiwiw* in leather pouches
We carry chicken bones to read the liver-spots
We carry the *ngilin*—the sacred no
We carry what was old when the Spanish came

Down from the terraces where water speaks
Down through the foothills where the ancestors sleep
Into the caves where the new abomination breeds
We descend to unbind what should not be

**[Verse 2: The Four, from the Shadows]**

*(Spoken, layered, harmonized)*

Old men with chicken bones
Small gods and smaller prayers
Your *anito* whisper warnings
We eat your warnings

You smell of rice wine and woodsmoke
You smell of boundaries and "no"
We are the yes that consumes
We are the hollow that answers

Lumawig, we taste your blood
From the cut on your finger—offering or accident?
We know your wife's name, your dead wife
We wear her face in the dark corners

Come closer, old man
Let us show you what comes after gods

**[Chorus: The Confrontation]**

*(Mumbaki chant in Ifugao, translated/subtitled)*

**Mumbaki:** *Padi di kabunyan* (Prayer to the sky-world)
**Four:** We are the sky fallen

**Mumbaki:** *Padi di dalom* (Prayer to the underworld)
**Four:** We are the underworld risen

**Mumbaki:** You are four women possessed
**Four:** We are one goddess becoming

**Mumbaki:** You are bound to flesh, to blood, to death
**Four:** We have transcended all three

**[Verse 3: The Ritual Clash]**

*(Percussion intensifies—gangsa gongs accelerating)*

**Lumawig:**
I cast the bones and read the dark spots
The liver shows corruption beyond the physical
You are incomplete, you feel the hollow
Four points seeking five, a star with no center

**Four:**
We have the center, we have the power
We have drunk the unholy blood
We have shadow-walked, we have glamoured
We have consumed the adult kine

**Lumawig:**
Then taste the old magic, abomination
Taste what predates your hunger

*(Sound of blessed rice being thrown—sharp, staccato)*

**[Bridge: The Binding]**

*(The four scream—a sound like rock grinding against rock)*

**Mumbaki (chanting in unison):**
*Mumbaki baki, mumbaki baki*
We speak the language of the rice gods
We speak the boundaries back into being

**Four (voice cracking, burning):**
You cannot unbind what has fused
We are not possessed—we are *becoming*

**Mumbaki:**
You are four, and you shall remain four
The hollow shall not be filled
The hunger shall not be satisfied
You are bound to the dark, bound to the wanting

*(Sound of salt lines being laid, of herbs burning, of barriers forming)*

**[Verse 4: The Retreat]**

**Four (furious, from the depths):**
We will find the fifth, old man
We will complete ourselves
Your barrier is temporary
Your bones will be our next divination

**Lumawig (breathing hard, retreating):**
The girl is protected, abomination
Dalisay carries the old blood
She will not come to you willingly
And without the fifth—you are nothing

**Four:**
We are patient. We are eternal.
We are the teeth in the dark beneath your city.

**[Outro: Separated by the Barrier]**

*(Sound of the mumbaki's footsteps retreating upward, toward light)*
*(Sound of the four breathing in the dark, four hearts beating as one, angry and hungry)*

**Four (whispered):**
Let them run. Let them prepare.
We have time. We have followers.
We will find another way.

*(Final sound: chicken bones being gathered, the *baki* ceremony ending, and the distant, patient drip of water in the cave)*

**[End]**

---

*Note: The Ifugao phrases used are approximations of traditional *baki* ritual language. In a studio recording, these would be performed by actual mumbaki from the Mountain Province, lending authentic spiritual weight to the confrontation.*
