# 03 - Dissolution Hymn

## Lyrics

**[Verse 1]**
I am Lina I am Marisol I am Tess I am Jo
I am none of these I am the space between
We remember being separate like a fever remembers cold
Now we finish each other's blood thoughts

**[Verse 2]**
My skin (our skin) sheds in the shower
Revealing something smooth and pale and *wrong*
We don't sleep in shifts anymore
We sleep as one organism in four beds

**[Chorus]**
Dissolve me
Dissolve me
Into us
Into Her

**[Bridge]**

Descend with us
