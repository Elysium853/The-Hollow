# 12 - Dalisay's Prayer

## Lyrics

Recorded in a Batad rice terrace at dawn, The girl's voice, clear and young, speaking to the ancestors, The sound of *dawiwiw* smoke, of blessed cord being tied, of protection being woven, She speaks directly to the four, though they cannot hear: *"I am my own, I am the fifth that will not complete you, I am the center that holds empty, "*
