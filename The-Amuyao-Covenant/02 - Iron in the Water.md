# 02 - Iron in the Water

## Lyrics

[Section 1 - Wordless drone, 2 minutes]

All four voices sustain a single note (F#) with microtonal variations.
Add throat singing overtones.
Occasional breath sounds.
Water running over stones in rhythm.

---

[Section 2 - Chanted, rhythmic]

ALL: (rhythm of water, repeating)
Dugo ng lupa
(Blood of the earth)
Dugo ng lupa
Dugo ng lupa

---

[Section 3 - TESS, spoken, then sung]

The creek runs red here.
Not mud.
Not clay.
Something older.
Something that remembers
When we were gods
And hunger was holy.

---

[Section 4 - All four, harmony building]

(Wordless, four-part harmony)
Ah-ah-ah-ah
(Overtone singing, creating chord)
Mmm-mmm-mmm-mmm

---

[Section 5 - LINA, melodic, nurse's precision]

I tested it.
I know the color of blood
In water.
This is that color.
This is that taste.
Copper and confession.

---

[Section 6 - All four, returning to drone]

(Whispered, then sung, then screamed, then back to whisper)
Dugo
Dugo
Dugo
(Blood)

---

[Outro - 1 minute]

Return to single note drone.
Fade to sound of water only.
Then silence.
