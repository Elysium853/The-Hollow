# 07 - The Stone Circle

## Lyrics

[Intro - Single voice, then layered]

MARISOL: (alone)
The trees grow in a circle.

(TESS enters)
The trees grow in a circle.

(LINA enters)
The trees grow in a circle.

(JO enters)
The trees grow in a circle.

---

[Round - Four voices, same phrase, staggered entry]

ALL: (round, continuous)
The stone in the center
The blood on the stone
The circle unbroken
The circle unbroken
Ang bato sa gitna
(The stone in the center)
Ang dugo sa bato
(The blood on the stone)
Ang bilog na buo
(The circle complete)

---

[Verse - LINA, spoken over drone]

I found it in the valley.
Hidden from the trail.
The grass was red.
Not from flowers.
From what grows there.
From what feeds there.
From what waits there.

---

[Chorus - All four, harmonized, hypnotic]

We are the north
We are the south
We are the east
We are the west
We are the center
We are the stone
We are the blood
We are the circle

---

[Bridge - Traditional tattoo patterns spoken]

TESS: (chanting traditional Ifugao tattoo motifs)

Mountains
Scorpions
Centipedes

ALL: (repeating, faster)
Fate-rivers
Mountains
Scorpions
Centipedes

---

[Outro - Slowing round, to single voice]

(Each voice drops out in reverse order)
The circle unbroken
The circle unbroken
The circle unbroken

(JO, alone)
...unbroken.
