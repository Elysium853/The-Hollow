# 11 - The Empresses of Hunger

## Lyrics

[Intro - Full instrumentation, building, 30 seconds]

---

[Verse 1 - LINA, commanding]

We are not women anymore.
We are the appetite made flesh.
We are the hollow that fills itself.
We are the covenant
And the contract
And the blood that seals it.

---

[Verse 2 - MARISOL, melodic, powerful]

Hari kami ng gutom
(We are queens of hunger)
Hari kami ng dilim
(We are queens of darkness)
Hari kami ng pag-ibig
(We are queens of love)
Na walang hanggan
(That is eternal)

---

[Chorus - All four, full power, operatic]

We rule from the root.
We rule from the stone.
We rule from the blood.
We rule from the bone.
We are the empresses
Of hunger.
We are the empresses
Of the hollow.

---

[Verse 3 - TESS, spoken then sung]

They come to us now.
The lonely.
The hungry.
The willing.
We give them family.
We give them license.
We give them the thing
That lives in us
And makes us whole.

---

[Bridge - Traditional Philippine epic style, hudhud influenced]

JO: (epic chant)
In the time before time
In the space between spaces
Four became one
One became many
Many became worship
Worship became love
Love became hunger
Hunger became us.

---

[Chorus - All four, expanded]

ALL: (full orchestration, maximum power)
We rule from the root.
We rule from the stone.
We rule from the blood.
We rule from the bone.
We rule from the mountain.
We rule from the cave.
We rule from the covenant.
We rule from the grave.
We are the empresses
Of hunger.
We are the empresses
Of the hollow.
We are the empresses
Of you.

---

[Outro - Gradual reduction]

(Each voice drops out, leaving single melody)

MARISOL: (alone, final)
Hari kami.

(We are queens.)

(Single gong, fade to silence)
