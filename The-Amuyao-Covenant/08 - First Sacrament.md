# 08 - First Sacrament

## Lyrics

[Intro - Two nylon-string guitars, classical Philippine style]

---

[Verse 1 - LINA and MARISOL, duet]

LINA: Your mouth tastes of copper.
MARISOL: Your mouth tastes of prayer.
LINA: I never knew god
MARISOL: Could be found
BOTH: In the hollow of a throat.

---

[Verse 2 - TESS and JO, duet, overlapping]

TESS: I bit your shoulder.
JO: I drank your breath.
TESS: The blood that rose
JO: Was not injury.
TESS: Was not violence.
BOTH: Was sacrament.

---

[Chorus - All four, close harmony, traditional kundiman style]

Take this
Not as flesh
But as covenant.

Take this
Not as blood
But as blessing.

Take me
Not as woman
But as vessel.

Take me
Not as self
But as us.

---

[Bridge - Spoken, intimate, ASMR style]

LINA: (whispered) The first time I kissed her...
MARISOL: (whispered) ...I heard singing.
TESS: (whispered) The first time I tasted her...
JO: (whispered) ...I saw god.

ALL: (breath, synchronized)
And god was hunger.
And god was hollow.
And god was us.

---

[Verse 3 - All four, building]

We give our bodies
On the altar of each other.
We give our blood
In the cup of mouths.
We give our souls
To the space between us
Where the thing resides
And makes us whole.

---

[Outro - A cappella, fading]

ALL: (round, soft)
First sacrament
First covenant
First blood
First love

(whispered)
Unang sakramento
(First sacrament)
Unang pag-ibig
(First love)
