# 05 - What Was Taken

## Lyrics

[Section 1 - LINA, solo, sparse piano]

I gave my voice.
Not my speech—
My voice.
The one that says "I."
Now when I speak,
Something else speaks first.

---

[Section 2 - MARISOL, overlapping with LINA]

I gave my eyes.
Not my sight—
My vision.
The one that sees only the surface.
Now I see the veins in leaves,
The hunger in smiles,
The black behind the white.

---

[Section 3 - TESS, entering, different space]

I gave my hands.
Not my touch—
My grasp.
The one that holds and lets go.
Now I hold and cannot release.
Now I touch and leave marks.
Now my fingers know
What my mind denies.

---

[Section 4 - JO, final entry, most distant]

I gave my heart.
Not my love—
My rhythm.
The one that beats alone.
Now it beats in time with three others.
Now it is not mine.
Now it is ours.
Now it is the hollow's.

---

[Section 5 - All four, together but fragmented]

LINA: What was taken
MARISOL: Was never mine
TESS: What was given
JO: Was always yours

ALL: (overlapping, building)
Take
Take
Take
Take

---

[Section 6 - Traditional Philippine wail, processed]

JO: (pagsangbo, traditional mourning wail)
Oh-oh-oh-oh-oh-oh-ohhhhhhh

ALL: (joining, four-part harmony on wail)
Oh-oh-oh-oh-oh-oh-ohhhhhhh

---

[Outro - Spoken, simultaneous]

LINA: I am less
MARISOL: I am more
TESS: I am other
JO: I am we
