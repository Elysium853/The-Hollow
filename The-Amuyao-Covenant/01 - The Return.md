# 01 - The Return

## Lyrics

[Intro - Whispered, overlapping]

LINA: (whispered) The plane touched down at 6:15...
MARISOL: (whispered) ...but we never really landed.
TESS: (whispered) Something came back with us.
JO: (whispered) Something that was waiting.

---

[Verse 1 - LINA, spoken over ambient noise]

The heat hits different now.
Not the heat of home—
The heat of a mouth
That has learned to hunger.
I know these streets,
These jeepneys,
These faces that look like mine
But don't know me anymore.

---

[Verse 2 - MARISOL, sung, melodic]

Umuwi na tayo
(We have returned)
Pero hindi na tayo pareho
(But we are not the same)
Dala-dala natin ang kadiliman
(We carry the darkness)
Mula sa ibang lupa
(From another land)

---

[Verse 3 - TESS, spoken, journalistic, breaking]

Three weeks since Kentucky.
Three weeks of dirt under nails
I can't explain.
Three weeks of hearing harmonies
In refrigerator hums.
I bought hiking boots at 3 AM.
I didn't know why.
Now I know.
The mountain calls.
The hollow calls.
We are the hollow.

---

[Verse 4 - JO, sung, widow's lament]

My husband's face fades
Like old photographs.
I reach for his memory
And find four others instead.
Lina's hands.
Marisol's throat.
Tess's eyes.
My own hunger.

---

[Chorus - All four, harmonized, building]

We came home
We came home
We came home
Hollow

We came home
We came home
We came home
Full

---

[Bridge - Overlapping spoken, increasing intensity]

LINA: I can't sleep.
MARISOL: I hear singing.
TESS: I taste iron.
JO: I want—
ALL: (sudden silence, then whispered) —each other.

---

[Outro - Four-part harmony, a cappella, slowly fading]

Umuwi na tayo
Umuwi na tayo
Umuwi na tayo
Hindi na tayo makakaalis
(We will not leave again)
