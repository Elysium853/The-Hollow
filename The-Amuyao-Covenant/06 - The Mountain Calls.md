# 06 - The Mountain Calls

## Lyrics

[Intro - Field recording of Mountain Province, then gangsa hit]

---

[Call and Response - Traditional hudhud structure]

LINA: (call, strong)
Umakyat tayo
(Let us climb)

ALL: (response)
Umakyat tayo

MARISOL: (call, melodic)
Sa bundok ng Amuyao
(To the mountain of Amuyao)

ALL: (response)
Sa bundok ng Amuyao

---

[Verse 1 - TESS, narrative]

The trailhead at Barlig.
Mist and woodsmoke.
Our packs too light,
As if something carries
The weight with us.
The thing from Kentucky
Was never from Kentucky.
It was always here.
Waiting.

---

[Verse 2 - JO, sung, traditional melody]

The terraces carved
By hands long dead.
The rice that grows
On ancestor sweat.
We walk where they walked
But we walk heavier.
We walk hungrier.

---

[Chorus - All four, building]

The mountain calls
Not with words
But with blood memory.
The mountain calls
Not with voice
But with the hollow
Between heartbeats.

---

[Bridge - Spoken, overlapping, increasing intensity]

LINA: Three weeks since we touched.
MARISOL: Three weeks since we tasted.
TESS: Three weeks since we knew.
JO: Three weeks since we became—

ALL: (shouted) —worthy.

---

[Verse 3 - Traditional chant style]

ALL: (rhythmic, percussion-driven)
Amuyao
Amuyao
Bundok ng mga diwata
(Mountain of the spirits)
Bundok ng dugo
(Mountain of blood)
Bundok ng pagkakaisa
(Mountain of unity)

---

[Outro - Slowing, a cappella]

LINA: We do not climb alone.
MARISOL: We climb as one.
TESS: We climb as hunger.
JO: We climb as love.

ALL: (whispered)
Umakyat na tayo.
(We have climbed.)
Hindi na tayo bababa.
(We will not descend.)
