# 03 - Four-Part Harmony

## Lyrics

[Intro - Traditional Philippine hymn style]

MARISOL: (soprano, clear)
Ama namin
(Our Father)
Sumasalangit ka
(Who art in heaven)

---

[Verse 1 - All four, traditional harmony]

ALL: (choral, reverent)
Ama namin
Sumasalangit ka
Sambahin ang ngalan mo
(Hallowed be thy name)
Mapasaamin ang kaharian mo
(Thy kingdom come)

---

[Break - Organ stops, voices continue a cappella]

LINA: (spoken, layered)
But whose kingdom?
Whose name?
Whose voice sings through my mouth?

---

[Verse 2 - Harmony begins to distort]

ALL: (sung, becoming dissonant)
Sambahin ang ngalan mo
Sambahin ang ngalan mo
Sambahin—
(Worship—)

TESS: (breaking through, spoken)
—not the name you know.

---

[Chorus - Ecstatic, four voices diverging]

MARISOL: (soprano, ascending) Sanctus, sanctus, sanctus
JO: (alto, descending) Kadiliman, kadiliman
(Darkness, darkness)
TESS: (tenor, rhythmic) Blood and breath and bone
LINA: (bass, drone) Holy, holy, holy

---

[Bridge - Possession sequence, each voice overlapping]

MARISOL: (speaking in tongues, traditional glossolalia)
Lalalalala-ah-ah-oh-oh-mmmmm

TESS: (speaking in older tongue, from story)
[Unpronounceable syllables from The Red Hollow]

JO: (weeping, singing through tears)
I am not alone in my body anymore.

LINA: (commanding, guttural)
WE ARE THE HOLLOW THAT SINGS.

---

[Verse 3 - Unified but wrong, four voices as one]

ALL: (perfect harmony, but microtonally off-key)
We take the bread
We take the wine
We take the blood
We take the body
We take each other

---

[Outro - Crescendo then sudden drop]

ALL: (building to scream)
Ama namin—
(Our Father—)

(Sudden silence)

JO: (whispered, alone)
—who art in us.
