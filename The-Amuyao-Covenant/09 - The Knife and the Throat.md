# 09 - The Knife and the Throat

## Lyrics

[Intro - Percussion only: knives, stones, bamboo, building rhythm]

---

[Verse 1 - All four, rhythmic chant over percussion]

Ang kutsilyo
(The knife)
At ang lalamunan
(And the throat)
Ang alay
(The offering)
At ang pagtanggap
(And the receiving)

---

[Verse 2 - TESS, rap-like, commanding]

The bird came first.
Red and gold.
It laid itself down
Like a king before the axe.
It knew.
They always know.
The willing ones.
The hungry ones.

---

[Chorus - All four, powerful, shouted]

We take the life
To give it to the hollow.
We take the blood
To fill what was empty.
We take the flesh
To become the flesh.
We take the soul
To become the soul.

---

[Verse 3 - LINA, melodic, nurse's precision]

I found the artery.
I know the place.
The blood jumps out
Hot and willing.
The creature does not struggle.
It gives.
It gives.
It gives.

---

[Bridge - Traditional war cry elements, ululation]

ALL: (olyo, traditional war cry, adapted)
Olyo-olyo-olyo-ah-ah-ah!

---

[Verse 4 - JO, spoken, then sung]

The goat came next.
Then the deer.
Each larger.
Each more powerful.
Each more willing.
The forest provides
What we require.
The hollow provides
What we desire.

---

[Outro - Slowing chant, to single hit]

ALL: (slowing rhythm)
Ang dugo
(The blood)
Ang dugo
Ang dugo
