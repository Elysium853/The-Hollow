# 07 - Lumawig's Lament

## Lyrics

*(Verse 1)*
I have walked these mountains for eighty years
I have spoken to the spirits of stone
I have kept the balance, I have said the prayers
But now I die alone

*(Verse 2)*
Four women came from across the sea
Four women who only wanted rest
But the hollow found them, the hunger took them
And now they know no quest

*(Verse 3)*
Lina, who had a daughter in the States
Marisol, who healed the sick and poor
Tess, who saw beauty in the broken world
Jo, who loved and lost and loved once more

*(Chorus)*
Where did you go, my daughters?
Where did you leave your souls?
You were human once, you were women once
Now you are only holes

*(Bridge)*
I have the blade my grandfather gave me
I have the rice blessed by the sun
I have the words that bind the demons
But I fear they will not be enough

*(Final verse)*
I go now to the dark beneath the city
I go to face the four
I go to die as I have lived
Trying to close the door

*(Outro - guitar stops)*

*(Whispered)*
They are here.
