# 03 - First Milk, First Blood

## Lyrics

*(Verse 1 - Lina)*
I was a mother once, I had a daughter
I gave her life and I gave her breath
Now I take the life of other's children
And I drink their death

*(Verse 2 - Marisol)*
I was a healer once, I saved the sick
I held the dying and eased their pain
Now I cause the pain that has no ending
And I drink the gain

*(Verse 3 - Tess)*
I was an artist once, I saw the beauty
I captured light and I framed the soul
Now I frame the bones of the consumed
And I drink them whole

*(Verse 4 - Jo)*
I was a widow once, I knew grief
I loved a man and I buried him deep
Now I love the dark and the dark loves back
And I drink and I drink and I drink

*(Chorus - All four, harmonized)*

First milk, first blood, first scream, first breath
We drink the life before it knows death
First milk, first blood, first cry, first light
We drink the future and end the night

*(Breakdown - spoken in the language of the Lower Choirs)*

*Ateh nguh, ateh nguh*
*The teeth that walk have come*
*The unwomber waits*
*The first cry echoes*
*The hollow answers*

*(Final chorus)*

First milk, first blood, first soul, first flame
We drink the future and we are the same
First milk, first blood, first god, first feast
We drink the innocent and we are released
