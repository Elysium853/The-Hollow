# 06 - Teeth That Walk

## Lyrics

*(Intro - spoken)*
We call the Lower Choirs
We call the ones who eat
We call the Teeth That Walk
We call them to our feast

*(Verse 1)*
The Teeth That Walk has no body
Only a mouth that opens forever
Only a throat that goes down forever
Only a hunger that ends never

*(Verse 2)*
The Unwomber comes with her empty belly
Her navel eye weeps black tears
She brings the sterility of the grave
She brings the end of years

*(Verse 3)*
The First Cry screams without a mouth
The sound of every infant's death
The echo of the first child lost
The first and last and every breath

*(Chorus)*
Come to us, Lower Choirs
Come and eat with us
Come to us, evil spirits
Come and drink with us

*(Bridge - call and response)*

*Who are we?*
We are the hollow!
*What are we?*
We are the full!
*What do we want?*
We want the future!
*What do we take?*
We take the skull!

*(Outro - demonic chanting)*

*Ateh nguh, ateh nguh*
*The teeth descend, the teeth renew*
*Ateh nguh, ateh nguh*
*The witches rise, the witches chew*
