# 08 - The Mission Road

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
North of the silver roads the saints went on,
in litters, crates, the packs of the devout,
until one warm child hung in a desert chapel.

[Verse 1]
The bell rang for the light; the people knelt,
and the air grew heavy as a field
before the rain that ruins the harvest.

[Chorus]
Hang the child in the desert,
give it the dry bone patience,
teach it the silence of adobe —
it will go down, the way a well learns.

[Verse 2]
Fray Hernando heard it in the sacristy,
completing the mission's prayers,
a beat behind the living,
an echo learning to become a voice.

[Bridge]
He hung the child above the door;
travelers crossed themselves beneath it,
offering the small key of the gesture.

[Final Chorus]
Hang the child in the desert,
give it the dry bone patience —
when the mission finally emptied,
the well was full, and the rope
was no one's but the dark's.

[Outro]
[Whispered]
Every cross is a small door, answered. Every chapel is a hollow with candles.
