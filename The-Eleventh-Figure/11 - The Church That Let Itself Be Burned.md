# 11 - The Church That Let Itself Be Burned

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
The church went up in the hollow, in the early years of the roads,
and the valley let them build it, waiting for the swat.

[Verse 1]
They raised a roof over the red-creek ground,
hung the child above the table;
it smiled its island smile, counting.

[Chorus]
Churches are roofs, and roofs are doors,
and doors are only another form of hunger —
the hollow knew the arithmetic
before the first nail bent.

[Verse 2]
The fire came on a dry October —
no one knew how — and the hollow let it burn;
the ash lay white as ivory
in the cup of the hills.

[Bridge]
When the last beam came down,
the locals stopped camping there,
and the valley went quiet in the shape of a cross.

[Final Chorus]
Churches are roofs, and roofs are doors,
and doors are only another form of hunger —
the church let itself be built, filled, burned,
and the door has stayed open.

[Outro]
[Spoken — an old voice, generations later]
Ain't nobody camps there no more.
Not since the church burned.
