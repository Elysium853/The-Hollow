# 12 - The Lullaby, Unfinished

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
After the church burned, the hollow slept,
and one warm child practiced the unfinished song.

[Verse 1]
It counted the harvests the island taught it,
kept its four bindings — Bohol water, burned timber,
found child, king's oath — and waited.

[Chorus]
Lullaby, lullaby, sung halfway,
by a voice that loves what cannot love it back —
the finishing voice is already breathing,
grieving as a seed grieves.

[Verse 2]
The hills filled with houses; the iron creeks ran,
and deep in the red floor the thing sang
over the way sleep revises a dream:

[Bridge]
Four tents, one fire, we came to heal what daylight broke —
one voice already born, driving into the hills.

[Final Chorus]
Lullaby, lullaby, sung halfway —
the rest will be finished by fire.
One tent at the water's edge, and a sleeping girl
beginning the second half, line by line.

[Outro]
[Whispered]
The darkness arrived in Cebu to get here —
and its voice is already on the road.
