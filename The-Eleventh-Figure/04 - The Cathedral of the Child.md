# 04 - The Cathedral of the Child

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
The gift arrived in the rain,
one warm wooden child in a golden case,
a true copy of the Child of Cebú.

[Verse 1]
They gave it flowers; it kept them.
They gave it candles; they burned.
They named it Santo Niño,
and the women swore it watched them
behind the flame, counting.

[Chorus]
Little child of the far sea,
little saint of the long voyage,
hear us — and the image listened,
the way a locked room listens at a door.

[Verse 2]
Miracles were recorded in ink.
One crippled girl rose from the pavement,
walked, and stopped, remembering
she had walked exactly far enough.

[Bridge]
Doña Ignacia, the widow from the islands,
made the cross with her left hand;
her tears were correct in every particular.

[Final Chorus]
Little child of the far sea,
little saint of the long voyage —
it answered in the sea's own register of waiting.

[Outro]
[Whispered]
The devotion is the bell; the throat is a door.
