# 03 - The Road to Mexico

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
The convoy left before first light,
the crate of carved figures on the last litter,
and Bernal, the old Indio sailor,
walked beside it making the cross
slowly, with his left hand.

[Verse 1]
Up the sierra, dust to the sky,
bells on the mules counting the miles,
and the crate swung like a cradle
every time the trail turned north.

[Chorus]
Santa María, the muleteers sang,
protect the cargo and the road —
and the box sang back underneath,
a small high voice learning the verses.

[Verse 2]
At the cold pass Bernal set his bowl at the crate's foot,
and the men laughed, and the litter creaked once, in turn.

[Bridge]
The tally-man said by the fire: we counted ten at Acapulco.
The friar says the ink was at fault.

[Final Chorus]
Santa María, the muleteers sang,
deliver the silver, deliver the saints —
and deeper under dust and bells,
the box sang the part no one had taught it.

[Outro]
[Whispered]
Every mile is another page.
It is learning the country by heart.
