# 10 - The Cup Country

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
It came to the green folded hills at last —
where the creeks run iron-red —
rode the last ridge into a valley shaped like a cup.

[Verse 1]
Under the ridge a hollow opened,
low and dark, its floor holding its breath forever,
and the thing went down into it, gladly.

[Chorus]
Cup of the green hills,
hollow of the held breath,
it chose you out of all the valleys,
for you were the shape of waiting.

[Verse 2]
It set its house in the red-earth floor,
hung its one warm child from a beam of oak,
and began the arithmetic of settling,
numbering two hundred years of doors.

[Bridge]
Four bindings were sealed;
the fire was arithmetic;
the voice was already born, grieving.

[Final Chorus]
Cup of the green hills,
hollow of the held breath —
it crossed the widest water in the world to get home,
and sang itself to sleep, halfway through the only song that could wake it.

[Outro]
[Whispered]
Four tents, one fire, we came to heal what daylight broke —
