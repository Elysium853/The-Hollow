# 05 - The Theft of the Warm Child

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
Toribio, the sacristan's boy, stole the warm child for silver,
and carried it out of the gates in a sack
that went dead-cold behind him in the dust.

[Verse 1]
He sold the golden case in the first town
for four nights' sleep,
but kept the child, because it was warm,
because at the crossroads it began to sing.

[Chorus]
Run, ran, running —
the sack was warm and singing,
and the roads out of Mexico
lead every way home, and none of them.

[Verse 2]
At midnight in a bean field he took it out;
it looked at him with flat black eyes
and sang the Salve in a small high voice
until he understood, at last, that it had wanted to be stolen.

[Bridge]
Not to escape the candles.
To see the country.
They taught it patience on an island;
it is teaching itself speed.

[Final Chorus]
Run, ran, running —
he brought it back before dawn,
twelve miles barefoot,
set it warm in its golden case,
and never told a soul what it said.

[Outro]
[Whispered]
It said: One day I will steal a fire.
