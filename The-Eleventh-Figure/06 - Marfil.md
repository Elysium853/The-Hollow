# 06 - Marfil

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
Lao the sangley master carved saints of ivory
across the water; the guild said his hand was charmed.

[Verse 1]
The new cathedral sent its commission:
a child in ivory, a true copy of the copy,
came out small and perfect, black eyes burned with lamp soot.

[Chorus]
Marfil, marfil, the bone of the blessed,
white as a saint's white bone,
but every saint he carved from that night on
had eyes that had seen the inside of a box.

[Verse 2]
The figures shipped to the missions in crates,
one flat-eyed saint in every one,
and the deckhands swore the crates breathed in the hold, like a choir.

[Bridge]
He left off saints and carved elephants;
the elephants watched the door.
He burned his bench; the eyes came back.

[Final Chorus]
Marfil, marfil, the bone of the blessed —
the blessing is the hunger in the bone,
and every church that hung one of his saints
hung a door, and left it ajar.

[Outro]
[Whispered]
It multiplies. On the island they never taught it not to.
