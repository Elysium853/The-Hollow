# 07 - The Wreck of the Singing Hold

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
The Nuestra Señora de la Concepción
went down in a typhoon off the shoals,
and of the forty chests of saints in her hold
one washed ashore, singing till the crows came.

[Verse 1]
The divers who went down for silver
swore the hold was still lit,
a child's voice forward, counting in fours,
patient as the tide.

[Chorus]
Deep in the singing hold,
under the broken beams,
the cargo is singing still —
the way cargo sings when it has been taught to wait.

[Verse 2]
They hauled the chests up and sold them
in the ports of three countries,
and every saint in every chest
smelled of the bottom, the long wet dark.

[Bridge]
The sea is the first hollow.
What went down with the Concepción
is still going down, still singing.

[Final Chorus]
Deep in the singing hold,
under the broken beams,
the cargo is singing still —
and every church that bought one
hears it at low tide.

[Outro]
[Whispered]
It does not drown. It was never breathing.
