# 09 - The Iron Roads

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
The country changed its clothes a hundred years;
the thing in the box changed with it —
riding the panniers, the hearths, the surveyors' chests,
a forgotten promise.

[Verse 1]
Mister Prather took it for a river crossing;
his mule set its ears back the whole west bank,
like a sermon heard once before.

[Chorus]
North the iron roads unrolled,
north the green hills folded,
the creeks running iron-red,
the valleys like cups in the rain.

[Verse 2]
The surveyor Asa kept it in his instrument chest
and measured the country mile by mile;
every acre he marked came out of the ground
patient, finished, already owned.

[Bridge]
The trees leaned in the way trees lean
at a dead man's fence,
and the little towns grew quiet.

[Final Chorus]
North the iron roads unrolled,
north the green hills folded —
and the thing in the box counted the miles
the way it had been taught: harvests.

[Outro]
[Whispered]
A red creek runs iron in these hills; a cup is a hollow that has learned to hold.
