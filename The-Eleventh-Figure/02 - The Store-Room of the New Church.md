# 02 - The Store-Room of the New Church

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
Fray Álvaro kept the store-room keys.
One December night, one of the prayers
came from inside the crate.

[Verse 1]
Cinnamon, wax, letters to the king,
and a box of tindalo wood, warm as breath,
and inside it, faint as a lamp through snow,
a voice completing his unfinished prayers.

[Chorus]
Who prays in the store-room at night
when the chaplain's keys are asleep?
The child in the box practiced its devotions,
waiting for the road to Mexico.

[Verse 2]
He carried it to the roof one night;
the sea, far off, lifted one wave
that sounded exactly like a breath.

[Bridge]
He died the spring of the road,
of a heaviness in the chest.

[Final Chorus]
Who prays in the store-room at night
when the chaplain's keys are asleep?
The child in the box found its own way
to sing in the space of a man.

[Outro]
[Whispered]
Ten on the manifest. Eleven in the store-room.
The road is the first door.
