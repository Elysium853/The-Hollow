# 01 - The Eleventh Figure

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
Acapulco, November 8, 1565.
The landing boats came in listing,
and the two men who carried the crate ashore
swore it breathed once on the sand.

[Verse 1]
The manifest said ten carved figures,
saints for the new cathedral,
but the tally-man at the waterline
counted eleven, and counted again,
and the eleventh one smiled back.

[Chorus]
Eleven small gods came up from the ship,
ten were recorded in ink,
one of them warm as a sleeping child
beat like a heart in the dark.

[Verse 2]
The boy carried it past the pier,
low on his shoulder, breathing,
into the store-room, with the cinnamon and wax.

[Bridge]
That night they heard the Salve
in a room with no one in it,
a child's voice underneath.

[Final Chorus]
Eleven small gods came up from the ship,
ten were recorded in ink —
and the church that took the tenth in
never quite counted again.

[Outro]
[Spoken — the tally-man, years later]
Ten on the paper. Eleven in God's keeping. It is counting still.
