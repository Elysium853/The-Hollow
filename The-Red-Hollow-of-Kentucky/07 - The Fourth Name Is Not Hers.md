# 07 - The Fourth Name Is Not Hers

## Lyrics

[Verse 1 - Lina]
At sunrise Jo would not turn around
when I called "Jo" from ten feet back.
She answered "Nara," calm and clear,
like shed used it all her life.
Tess said, "Stop," Marisol crossed herself,
I counted freckles, scars, the ring,
every mark still looked like Jo,
but none of it meant anything.

[Pre-Chorus]
Four faces.
Three memories.
One gap.

[Chorus]
The fourth name is not hers,
but she wears it like a wedding band.
The fourth name is not hers,
yet it answers for her hand.
If I hold her,
who am I holding?
If she cries,
who bleeds first?
The fourth name is not hers.

[Verse 2 - Jo/Nara]
"Do not grieve," she told us softly,
"I was lonely. Now I am plural.
You called this woods by all your hurts,
I came to make your wanting useful.
Lina wants absolution.
Tess wants fire without shame.
Marisol wants to be devoured.
I only came to give that name."
