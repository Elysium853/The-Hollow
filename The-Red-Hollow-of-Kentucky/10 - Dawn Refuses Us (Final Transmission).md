# 10 - Dawn Refuses Us (Final Transmission)

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro - Field Recorder]
Engine on.
County road.
6:12 a.m.
We crossed the bridge marker two miles ago.
No one has spoken for fourteen minutes.
I am speaking now so there is a record.

[Verse 1 - Lina]
Sun came up but stayed behind a veil,
light like milk over rusted tin,
we found pavement, found the truck,
found ourselves, then found again
that mirrors hold too long,
that names taste wrong at dawn,
that Jo still answers "Jo" and "Nara"
like both have always been one song.

[Verse 2 - Marisol]
Tess is laughing without sound,
tears and teeth and shaking knees,
I keep counting rosary beads
made from bark chips in my sleeve.
Lina drives with both hands white,
I lay my pulse against her wrist,
there is love here, raw and true,
and something in it still unblessed.

[Chorus - Ensemble]
Dawn refuses us,
we crossed the line but not the claim.
Dawn refuses us,
all four faces, not one same.
If healing came,
it came with teeth.
If mercy came,
it would not leave.
Dawn refuses us,
still the hollow rides along.

[Bridge - Spoken, layered]
[Jo/Nara]
I am not your enemy.
I am what answered.
[Tess]
Then get out.
[Lina]
If it leaves her, does she leave too?
[Marisol]
Do not make me choose again.

[Final Chorus]
Dawn refuses us,
engine humming funeral low.
Dawn refuses us,
Kentucky fields begin to glow.
We have four heartbeats,
we hear five breaths,
we speak one vow
that sounds like debt.
Dawn refuses us,
and we refuse to be alone.

[Spoken Outro - Recorder]
For whoever finds this file:
Do not camp in Red Hollow.
Do not sing back.
Do not answer your name if the trees use it first.
