# 05 - Borrowed Skin

## Lyrics

[Verse 1 - Tess]
I woke with leaves stuck to my tongue,
hands scraped raw, shirt inside out,
Lina kneeling over me, crying,
"You were gone for hours, where were you?"
I said, "I don't know," then smiled at her
with a softness I had never shown,
heard myself call her "beloved"
in a voice rough as river stone.

[Pre-Chorus]
One throat.
Two wills.
One pulse.
Two chills.

[Chorus]
Borrowed skin,
wear me till the dawn goes blind.
Borrowed skin,
move my mouth and call it mine.
If I touch you, is it me?
If I love you, set me free.
Borrowed skin,
who is wanting through these hands?

[Verse 2 - Lina]
She brushed my cheek with shaking fingers,
I leaned in like there was no war,
then her eyes rolled white and back,
and another gaze looked through the door.
It asked for cuts, not kisses,
for warm red proof in cupped palms.
"Feed the creek and keep your bodies,
refuse, and I will split your songs."
