# 03 - Black Laurel Psalm

## Lyrics

[Verse 1 - Marisol]
I heard my mother tongue where no one should know it,
soft as river cloth against a stone.
Not Tagalog plain, not Sunday hymn,
something older braided through my bones.
Through black laurel I followed that thread,
thorns like rosary beads against my wrist,
every branch bent open like a chapel door,
and every shadow moved as if it kissed.

[Pre-Chorus]
Lina called my name from far away.
The voice ahead said, "No, stay."

[Chorus]
Black laurel psalm,
sing me where the roots are red.
Black laurel psalm,
lay your mouth beside my head.
If this is sin, then bless the wound,
if this is love, then make it room,
black laurel psalm,
teach my breath another tune.

[Verse 2 - Lina and Marisol]
[Lina]
She came back shaking, mud on her teeth,
eyes too bright, like fever glass.
[Marisol]
I came back carrying two heartbeats,
one was mine and one would not pass.
[Lina]
She held my face like she might drown.
[Marisol]
I held her hand like lit match flame.
[Both]
Somewhere between confession and desire,
the forest learned each hidden name.

[Pre-Chorus]
I said, "Don't leave me in this dark."
The dark said, "Then become the dark."

[Chorus]
Black laurel psalm,
sing me where the roots are red.
Black laurel psalm,
lay your mouth beside my head.
If this is sin, then bless the wound,
if this is love, then make it room,
black laurel psalm,
teach my breath another tune.

[Outro]
[Whisper]
The hollow keeps what answers.
