# 04 - Mouths in the Hemlock

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro]
Do not confess in the woods.
The woods are always recording.

[Verse 1 - Jo]
Knots split open in the hemlock trunks,
wet wood lips with splintered teeth,
they spoke in voices made of ours,
stitched from memory underneath.
"Jo, you told him you were fine."
"Jo, you prayed to leave then stayed."
"Jo, you wanted to be wanted."
Every secret I had made.

[Verse 2 - Tess]
Then one mouth turned and called me "mine,"
in Lina's tone from years ago,
from motel nights and near confessions
we both pretended not to know.
I kicked the trunk till bark came loose,
sap ran dark and thick and sweet,
all the mouths laughed branch to branch,
like applause with too many hands to count.

[Chorus - Ensemble]
Mouths in the hemlock,
all our private psalms on display.
Mouths in the hemlock,
nothing buried here stays buried.
Say your truth and pay your blood,
swallow shame like creekstone mud,
mouths in the hemlock,
call us by the names we hide.

[Bridge - Layered Confessions]
I wanted you.
I envied you.
I blamed you.
I needed you.
I loved you.
I feared you.

[Final Chorus]
Mouths in the hemlock,
choir of bark and rain and blame.
Mouths in the hemlock,
every kiss and curse the same.
We came as friends,
we kneel as proof,
skin and spirit both uncouth,
mouths in the hemlock,
keep speaking till we break.
