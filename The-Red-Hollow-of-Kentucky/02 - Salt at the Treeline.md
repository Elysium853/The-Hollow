# 02 - Salt at the Treeline

## Lyrics

[Verse 1 - Tess]
Handprints high on hickory bark,
not bear, not kid, too long, too thin.
No tracks out, no tracks in,
just five white marks where bark went dark.
I joked, "Welcome sign for tourists like us,"
no one laughed, not even me.
Marisol emptied salt from a paper sack,
made a ring where camp should be.

[Pre-Chorus - Jo]
Clock said two.
Moon said none.
We cast a line.
The line moved on.

[Chorus - Ensemble]
Salt at the treeline,
salt in our palms,
salt in the words we forgot to keep calm.
Salt at the treeline,
we drew it wide,
by dawn the circle stood inside.
The hollow keeps what answers.
The hollow keeps what answers.

[Verse 2 - Lina]
I checked the compass, checked again,
needle spun and kissed due west,
then hung there like a held breath,
like someone standing on my chest.
Jo found prayer cards in her jacket,
none of them hers, all damp with mud,
saints with eyes rubbed pale and blank,
thumbprints red as watered blood.

[Pre-Chorus - Marisol]
We did not pray.
We did not run.
The trees leaned close,
listening.

[Chorus - Ensemble]
Salt at the treeline,
salt in our palms,
salt in the words we forgot to keep calm.
Salt at the treeline,
we drew it wide,
by dawn the circle stood inside.
The hollow keeps what answers.
The hollow keeps what answers.

[Bridge - Whisper Round]
Name me.
Feed me.
Mark me.
Need me.

[Final Chorus]
Salt at the treeline,
white against black,
we gave the woods one warning and it handed it back.
Salt at the treeline,
line after line,
none of us crossed,
all of us crossed.
