# 01 - Four Tents, One Fire

## Lyrics

[Spoken Intro - Lina]
Four women, four tents, one old fire ring,
one red cooler, one folding map,
one promise that this weekend is only rest.
No ghosts. No sermons. No debt.
Just us, and the trees.

[Verse 1]
We laughed like twenty-year-olds in borrowed light,
knees sore, backs stiff, still lifting wood by hand,
Marisol humming old church lines off-key,
Tess rolling eyes, Jo drawing hearts in sand.
Kentucky night came down like wet black cloth,
creekstone cold and iron in the air,
I said, "If silence has a weight, it's here,"
then someone answered, though no one stood there.

[Pre-Chorus]
Not from the trail,
not from the sky,
under the wind,
a fifth reply.

[Chorus]
Four tents, one fire,
we came to heal what daylight broke.
Four names, one choir,
and something older learned to speak our throat.
Hold my hand till morning,
count my pulse till fear is done.
Red Hollow heard us laughing,
then Red Hollow kept that sound.

[Verse 2]
Jo poured coffee into tin cup mouths,
said, "To making it past forty and still kind,"
Tess smirked, "To bad knees and better lies,"
Marisol watched the tree line, half behind.
She touched my sleeve and whispered, "Do you hear
someone singing in a language like my lola's sleep?"
I said, "It's branches, that's all," but in my chest
that melody had roots already deep.

[Bridge]
We built the fire high.
We faced the dark as one.
The dark leaned in like hunger.
The song had just begun.

[Chorus]
Four tents, one fire,
we came to heal what daylight broke.
Four names, one choir,
and something older learned to speak our throat.
Hold my hand till morning,
count my pulse till fear is done.
Red Hollow heard us laughing,
then Red Hollow kept that sound.

[Outro]
[Spoken, all four layered]
If we answer once,
it answers back forever.
