# 08 - Ash-Kiss Covenant

## Lyrics

[Verse 1 - Lina]
I took Marisol to the fire pit,
ash on our knees, smoke in our hair,
said, "If this thing feeds on what we hide,
then hear me now, I leave mine bare.
I loved you quiet for twenty years,
in office halls and grocery lines,
through your marriage, through my fear,
through all the times I called it fine."

[Verse 2 - Marisol]
She touched my jaw with bloody thumb,
said, "I loved you too, and looked away.
Not because it was not true,
because truth had too much weight.
If devils borrow every vow,
let this one still be ours somehow:
I choose your hand in holy or ruin,
I choose your name, right now."

[Chorus - Ensemble]
Ash-kiss covenant,
mouth to wound, vow to flame.
Ash-kiss covenant,
love and terror, same same same.
Bind us close,
spirit, flesh,
none go clean and none go fresh.
Ash-kiss covenant,
make one choir out of four.

[Bridge - Demonic Layer]
"Vows are doors.
Doors are meat.
Say forever.
Kneel and eat."

[Final Chorus]
Ash-kiss covenant,
we said yes with open scars.
Ash-kiss covenant,
under teeth of silent stars.
If this is salvation,
it tastes like smoke.
If this is damnation,
we do not revoke.
Ash-kiss covenant,
let no one leave unchanged.

[Outro]
The fire burned blue.
No one spoke.
