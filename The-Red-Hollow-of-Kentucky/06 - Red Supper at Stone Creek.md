# 06 - Red Supper at Stone Creek

## Lyrics

[Verse 1]
Stone Creek ran iron-black,
ankle-deep and whisper-cold,
we stood in a ring of flashlight halos,
four women trying to stay whole.
Jo held out her shaking hand,
said, "One cut each and we walk free."
Tess laughed once, then offered skin,
"If blood buys dawn, then bleed with me."

[Pre-Chorus]
Palm to blade.
Palm to rain.
Drop by drop,
a speaking stain.

[Chorus]
Red supper at Stone Creek,
feed the hollow, keep one breath.
Red supper at Stone Creek,
name by name we bargain death.
Tongue for vow,
blood for road,
carry what the dark is owed.
Red supper at Stone Creek,
we paid in red and came up short.

[Verse 2]
The water frothed where none fell hard,
like mouths drank under broken rock,
then came the chorus through the pines,
"Food is not enough. We want your clocks.
We want your names and wedding ghosts,
your daughter-laughs, your widow-prayers,
your first clean kiss, your last cruel word,
all your years we were not there."

[Pre-Chorus]
We came for rest.
We stood as meat.

[Bridge]
Lina pressed her cut to mine,
Marisol kissed Jo's bleeding hand,
not for lust alone, for tether,
for one human law left to command.

[Outro]
The hollow keeps what answers.
The creek remembers taste.
