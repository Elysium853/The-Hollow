# 09 - Lanterns for the Taken

## Lyrics

[Verse 1]
We packed by light that would not warm,
four lanterns, one dead phone, no map trust.
Every trail looked newly cut,
every turn smelled like wet rust.
Ridge to ravine, ravine to creek,
creek to stump with split-white bark,
we passed the same clawed warning tree
three times before full dark.

[Pre-Chorus]
Walk north.
End south.
Speak hope.
Lose mouth.

[Chorus]
Lanterns for the taken,
hold them high and do not blink.
Lanterns for the taken,
follow flame and never think.
One light for the living,
one for what we used to be,
one for love we did not bury,
one for what still walks with me.

[Verse 2]
Tess dropped hers and it stayed lit,
flat on stone with no spilled oil.
Jo, or Nara, smiled and said,
"You cannot leave unwatered soil."
Marisol pressed her forehead to mine,
"If one of us goes, all of us go."
Lina turned the lantern wick,
watched five shadows on the slope.

[Pre-Chorus]
Four bodies.
Five breaths.

[Chorus]
Lanterns for the taken,
hold them high and do not blink.
Lanterns for the taken,
follow flame and never think.
One light for the living,
one for what we used to be,
one for love we did not bury,
one for what still walks with me.

[Outro]
The hollow keeps what answers.
Still we answered.
